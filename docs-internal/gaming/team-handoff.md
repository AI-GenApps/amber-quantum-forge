# Per-team implementation handoff

The repository integration owner maintains the registry, Pub workspace, app manifests, native project generation, developer commands, affected detection, Knip, CI, and readiness records. The following owners continue from the frozen paths and source manifests:

| Owner scope | Path | Foundation smoke already available | Remaining MVP work |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Merge Relay](handoffs/merge-relay) | `apps-native/games/merge_relay`, `apps-native/games/packages/merge_rules` | deterministic merge/replay, accessible controls, save recovery and idempotent Flame cleanup | Complete the source-indexed board, relay, progression, economy, multiplayer, Android rerun and release acceptance tests. |
| [Pocket Biome](handoffs/pocket-biome) | `apps-native/games/pocket_biome`, `apps-native/games/packages/biome_rules` | injected-clock growth/breeding, bounded settlement persistence, accessible pot selection and lifecycle recovery | Complete genetics, content, progression, notifications, economy, Android rerun and source-indexed validation. |
| [Sixty-Second Heist](handoffs/sixty-second-heist) | `apps-native/games/sixty_second_heist`, `apps-native/games/packages/heist_rules` | bounded simulation trace, accessible responsive controls, ordered draft persistence and cleanup | Complete tools, solver, editor, content, progression, economy, Android rerun and source-indexed validation. |
| [Meme Court](handoffs/meme-court) | `apps-native/games/meme_court`, `apps-native/games/packages/court_rules` | round transitions, ballot safety, moderation state, authored caption-choice/vote/result path, widget coverage and QA-accepted readable Android route/device/recovery evidence | Complete approved prompt/content quantity, live room boundaries, moderation operations, web voting, economy and source-indexed validation. |
| [SnapQuest / Peeklings](handoffs/snapquest) | `apps-native/games/snapquest`, `apps-native/games/packages/snapquest_rules` | recognition contract, bounded camera seam, camera denial, desk fallback, lifecycle/capture gating tests and QA-accepted readable Android camera/desk/recovery/relaunch route evidence | Calibrate descriptors on a physical device, then complete privacy/consent, quests, collections, content, economy and source-indexed validation. |
| API isolation | `packages/api/src/games/**` | implemented per-app route, signed-token, storage, snapshot, and cross-app authorization boundary | Keep the local fail-closed store until an approved persistence owner and migration plan exist; keep production migration and client multiplayer integration separate. |

Every team must keep pure rules free of Flutter/device dependencies, use the registry stable ID, preserve save namespaces during migrations, inject clocks/RNG where outcomes are deterministic, and add replay/content fixtures for the current approved source revision. Optional SDKs and permissions are enabled only after an owner supplies the adapter, tests, privacy record, and registry state transition.

The five source-specific handoffs are linked in the Gaming documentation navigation and each links its own provenance manifest. The cross-app precedence and genuine source conflicts are recorded in [`requirements-reconciliation.md`](requirements-reconciliation.md); unresolved conflicts must be recorded there and in the relevant source manifest rather than inferred.

Android final QA owns the accepted postmigration smoke evidence on physical `SM-A525F` serial `RZ8R32EAB7T` (Android 14) and its artifact/device/route joins. The first-three unchanged APK hashes are explained by tree-shaken unused migration code. Remaining physical QA work is limited to SnapQuest descriptor calibration; Court's broader membership, moderation, service and social requirements remain full-MVP owner gates. The retained five-app signed/install/play iOS baseline is historical evidence; further iOS QA is paused until after Google Play publication. The five requirement ledgers remain the source of truth for MVP scope, and no game is Play-published or full-MVP complete.
