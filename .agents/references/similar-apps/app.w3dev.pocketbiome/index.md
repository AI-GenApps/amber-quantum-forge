## Genre benchmarks

- The Runaway studio pair (Hatch Dragons, Flutter Starlight) sets the quality bar for cozy breeding collectors: calm pacing, no punishing timers, cosmetic monetization.
- NimbleBit's Pocket Frogs proves long-tail longevity for breeding collectors (a decade-old franchise still charting in its niche).

## Takeaways

- Pocket Frogs is the direct comp for store listing positioning: compare against it in marketing copy, not merge/idle games.
- "No plant death for absence" (our PB guarantee) is a stated anti-pattern versus older care sims that punish lapses — call it out in the listing.
- Read-only friend visits and gifting are rare in this genre (most are fully solo) — the PB-06/PB-07 requirements are a real differentiator once shipped.
