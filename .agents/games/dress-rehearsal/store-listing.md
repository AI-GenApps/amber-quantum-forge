## Long description

Dress Rehearsal is a calm, clever styling puzzle. A client arrives with a creative brief: warm
colours only, nothing newer than the seventies, something waterproof, a budget to respect. Your
job is to pull the right pieces from the closet and lay them out on a flat-lay lookbook board.

Every rule is on the card and turns green the moment you meet it. A harmony meter explains what
works (colour scheme, neutrals, dress code, era, season) and what clashes. Meet every rule for two
stars; make it sing for three. When the board looks right, save it to your gallery or export it as
an image and share it.

- 40 handmade challenges across six themes: Riviera Summer, Rainy City, Gala Night, Mountain Weekend, Studio Avant-garde and Vintage 70s.
- 48 pieces to collect, from sun hats and trench coats to velvet gowns, puffer jackets and platform boots.
- Six recurring clients with strong opinions, and a bond that grows with every look.
- A fresh Daily Fitting every day, generated for your date, with a streak to keep.
- No body, no mannequin, no timer. Undo as much as you like.
- Export your board with four frames and share it.
- Works offline. No ads, no accounts, no purchases, nothing collected.
- Readable fonts, large touch targets, reduced motion and high contrast options.

## Data safety (Google Play) and privacy nutrition label (App Store)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does the app collect or share user data? | No data collected. No data shared. |
| Personal info, location, contacts, photos, audio, files, diagnostics, identifiers | None collected. |
| Data processed on device only | Progress, settings, gallery boards and the daily streak in local storage (Capacitor Preferences). Exported images are generated on the device and shared only when the player chooses. |
| Network access | None. The app makes no network requests. |
| Data deletion | Uninstalling removes the save; Settings has "Reset progress". |
| Third-party SDKs | None that collect data (Capacitor core and first-party plugins only). |
| App Store tracking (ATT) | Not used. |

A privacy policy URL is still required by both stores even with no data collected: see
