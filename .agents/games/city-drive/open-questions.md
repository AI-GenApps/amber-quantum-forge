| 12 | **Adaptive quality on a real device (task 15).** The governor (`src/perf`) is proven with synthetic frame-time streams and a NullEngine host, not with a real GPU. On the device, with `?fps`, check at task 22: (a) the frame rate holds near 30 or 60 on a low-end Android WebView and the tier settles within about 15 s without flipping; (b) tiers 3 to 7 (scaling 2.0, fog to 60%, traffic to 30%) still look acceptable; (c) the 20 ms / 33 ms thresholds, the 10 s recovery and the 20 s probation lock feel right (all constants are at the top of `src/perf/governor.ts`); (d) a 3x phone looks sharp enough at the 2x render cap. | user | task 22 |
| 13 | **Frozen materials with changing fog, on real WebGL (task 15).** Materials are frozen and the governor changes `scene.fogStart/fogEnd` at runtime. Fallback if it fails: do not freeze materials, keep only the mesh freezing. | user | task 16, task 22 |
| 14 | **CPU gate is machine-relative (task 15).** `test/fixtures/perf-baseline.json` was recorded on this build server (0.13 ms per fixed step, 5 to 10x slower on a phone, target at most 4 ms). The gate allows 2x the baseline scaled by a calibration loop, plus a 2 ms ceiling. If CI flakes, re-record with `bun apps-games/city-drive/scripts/record-perf-baseline.ts`. A real device number comes at task 22. | user | task 22 |
| 15 | **JS size margin is thin (task 15).** The app JS loaded at start is 1,567,122 B: inside 1.5 MiB by 5.7 KB, over 1.5 MB decimal. Any new dependency or large module will break `report-bundle.ts`. Decide at task 16 whether 1.5 MiB is the intended reading; if not, the next trims are lazy-loading the garage and the settings/onboarding screens. | user | task 16 |
