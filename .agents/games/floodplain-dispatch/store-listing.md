## Long description

Floodplain Dispatch is a calm, clever puzzle about water. A river runs through your town. The
forecast says a surge is coming. You have a handful of canals, pumps, sandbags, sluice gates and
reservoirs, and a budget to spend before the season begins.

Place your pieces, run the season, and watch the river decide. Every house needs a drink. Every
field needs a soak. No house may get wet feet. Spend less than the dispatcher's par and you earn
the third star.

- 40 hand-built maps across four regions: the Valley, the Delta, the Highlands and the Estuary.
- Each region teaches a new idea: rain surges, sluice-gate timing, reservoirs and droughts, tides.
- A fresh Daily Dispatch every day, generated for your date, with a streak to keep.
- Rewind any season and edit your plan. No lives, no timers, no pressure.
- Seven map themes to unlock as you earn stars.
- Hints when you are stuck, paid for with coins you earn by playing.
- Works offline. No ads, no accounts, no purchases, nothing collected.
- Readable fonts, large touch targets, reduced motion and high contrast options.
