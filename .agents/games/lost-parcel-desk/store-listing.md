## Long description

It is 1974 and the river has been through the Ketterly post office. The labels are gone, the sacks are
soggy, and the old clerk has left you the desk. Every lost parcel holds a handful of small, ordinary
things. Who do they belong to?

Pick up each object, turn it over, hold the magnifier to it. Pin the clues that matter to your board and
link them to the townsfolk they fit, or rule people out. When you are sure, hand the parcel over.

- 12 hand-written cases in three chapters, each solved in 5 to 8 minutes.
- 49 evidence objects, 12 townsfolk with their own streets, pastimes, habits and pets.
- Clues that work in pairs, clues that rule people out, and clues that are only there for charm.
- A water-stained ledger that tells the story of the flood night, one fragment at a time.
- A town map that fills in as you deliver.
- A new Daily Parcel every day, generated for your date, with a streak to keep.
- Stamps to collect and replay for. Five desk themes to unlock.
- A Clerk's Almanac that explains every kind of clue, so every case is fair.
- Works offline. No ads, no accounts, no purchases, nothing collected.
- Readable fonts, large touch targets, reduced motion and high contrast options.
