## Long description

The Last Window is a cosy cafe-bookshop at the edge of a harbour town, and for the next two weeks it is
yours. The big arched window faces the sea. Your great-aunt left it to you, and she left the lamp too.

Every day is a short shift of three or four minutes. Regulars and passers-by sit at your counter and ask
for a drink, maybe a pastry, maybe a book to suit their mood. Tap the steps to make the tea and coffee,
warm a pastry, pick the right book from the shelf and hand it over before the tea goes cold. Happy
customers tip, and the regulars start to tell you their stories.

- 14 days of story: eight regulars with five-scene stories each, 40 scenes of original writing.
- Choices that matter: every regular has two possible endings, and your answers decide which one.
- Plan tomorrow: choose which pastries and books to stock, because shelf space is limited.
- Six drinks, five pastries and five books to unlock day by day.
- Eight pieces of decor to buy, each with a cosy bonus.
- Rain, morning and evening shifts with their own light and mood.
- Late Shifts after the story ends, with a fresh set of regulars every evening and a streak to keep.
- Works offline. No ads, no accounts, no purchases, nothing collected.
- Readable fonts, large touch targets, reduced motion, high contrast, a relaxed pace option and a
  recipe guide.
