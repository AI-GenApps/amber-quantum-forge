## Setting and tone

Port Merrow, a small harbour town. The Last Window is the last building on Quay Lane before the sea
wall, a cafe-bookshop with one big arched window facing the water. Your great-aunt Marit lit a lamp in
that window every evening so late boats could find the harbour; she left the shop to you three weeks
ago. Lantern Night, on Day 14, is the town festival where paper lanterns are floated for everyone who
works on the water, and where the shop lamp is lit last. Tone: warm, quiet, small stakes, kind humour,
no villains, no violence, no romance beyond friendship. Plain language (about grade 6 reading level).
You are "You": the player speaks in choices and short lines; Marit's old notes appear as captions.

## The 8 regulars

All eight are drawn in the same painterly style, same bust framing and scale (portrait 3:4,
shoulders-up, transparent), with three expressions each: neutral, happy, sad (same face, hair and
outfit; only the expression changes). Slot ids: `customer-N-neutral`, `customer-N-happy`,
`customer-N-sad`.

Choice format: each option has a short label (at most 8 words), a lean (`a` or `b`) and 2 reaction
beats. Ending rule in `product.md`. Scene gifts: scenes 1 to 4 pay 5 coins, scene 5 pays 15.

### 1. Odile Marsh (`customer-1`) - "The Light on Skerry Point"

- Look: 74, weathered kind face, silver hair in a low bun, round reading glasses on a cord, navy
  fisherman's cardigan with horn buttons.
- Likes: Harbour Black, Salted Butter Roll; moods wistful, comforting. Visits: Days 1, 3, 6, 9, 11.
- Scenes: (1) "The Chair by the Glass": she asks for the seat nearest the window and says she kept Skerry
  Point light for forty years and could always see this lamp across the water. (2) "Forty Years of
  Weather": she brings a worn logbook and reads one storm entry; the council will take the old lens down
  after Lantern Night. (3) "A Brass Letter": a council letter asks whether the lens should go to the
  museum or be lit one last time; she cannot decide. (4) "Storm Glass": on a rainy day she tells how
  Marit once waved a lamp from this window to guide a lost boat past the rocks. (5) "The Light on Skerry
  Point": the ending.
- Choice c1 (scene 3), "What should Odile tell the council?": a "Ask to light it one more night"; b "Offer
  the lens to the museum".
- Endings: A "Lit One More Night": the council agrees, and on Lantern Night the beam sweeps the quay with
  Odile at the switch. B "The Lens on the Shelf": the lens goes to the museum, a brass copy hangs in the
  Last Window, and Odile keeps watch from her chair.

### 2. Tobias Venn (`customer-2`) - "The Marigold"

- Look: 27, tall and lean, freckled, sandy hair under a knit cap, rolled-sleeve navy overalls with oil
  stains, easy grin.
- Likes: Tide Latte, Cinnamon Knot; moods funny, adventurous. Visits: Days 1, 4, 6, 9, 12.
- Scenes: (1) "Grease on the Page": he leaves a thumbprint on a book, apologises, and says he keeps the
  old ferry Marigold running. (2) "The Cargo Line Letter": the Northern Cargo Line offers him a job on big
  ships, starting the day after Lantern Night. (3) "A Spare Part": the Marigold needs a pump part nobody
  sells; a sketch in one of Marit's almanacs shows how to make one. (4) "Night Shift on the Quay": he works
  late on the engine and tells why the ferry matters (his mother crossed on it every day for work).
  (5) "The Marigold": the ending.
- Choice c1 (scene 2), "What do you tell Tobias?": a "The Marigold needs you here"; b "Big ships will not
  wait for you".
- Choice c2 (scene 4), "What do you bring Tobias?": a "Soup, and a hand with the torch"; b "A kind
  letter of reference".
- Endings: A "The Last Crossing": he stays one more season and the Marigold carries the lantern crossing.
  B "Postcards on the Corkboard": he takes the cargo job after training his apprentice, and his postcards
  pile up by the till.

### 3. Priya Anand (`customer-3`) - "The Tide Pool Guide"

- Likes: Fog Tea, Pear Tart; moods curious, comforting. Visits: Days 1, 3, 5, 8, 10.
- Scenes: (1) "Notes in the Margin": she writes at the window and asks for books on the local coast.
  (2) "The Sea Hare": she found a sea slug she thinks is unrecorded here and fears she is wrong.
  (3) "Low Tide, Muddy Boots": back from the shore with mud to the knees, she must decide what to do with her find.
  (4) "Cold Hands, Warm Cup": nervous about her talk, she practises it for you and you lend her a funny
  book. (5) "The Tide Pool Guide": the ending.
- Choice c1 (scene 3), "What should Priya do with her find?": a "Show the whole town on Lantern Night";
  b "Write to the institute first".
- Endings: A "The Touch Table": she runs a tide-pool touch table on the quay and children line up. B "The
  Letter From Away": the institute confirms a rare record, and she dedicates the guide to the shop.

### 5. Henrik Ahlgren (`customer-5`) - "Two Stalls, One Quay"

- Likes: Midnight Mocha, Ginger Loaf; moods funny, comforting. Visits: Days 2, 5, 7, 10, 13.

### 6. Dr. Leona Castellane (`customer-6`) - "House Call Hours"

- Likes: Honey Chamomile, Cinnamon Knot; moods comforting, wistful. Visits: Days 2, 5, 8, 11, 13.

### 7. Abel Quill (`customer-7`) - "The Book That Came Home"

- Likes: Fog Tea, Salted Butter Roll; moods curious, wistful. Visits: Days 3, 4, 7, 10, 12.

## Tutorial plan (Day 1, steps shown by the onboarding task)

Six scripted customers on Day 1 (regulars 1, 2, 3 plus 3 walk-ins), morning weather. Step list:
(1) Marit's note on the way into the shift: "Keep the lamp lit"; (2) tap Tobias's seat; (3) make a Harbour
Black (cup, brew, pour) with the glowing guide; (4) tap Tobias to serve and see the tip; (5) Priya wants a
Salted Butter Roll: plate, warm (start the oven, then make a walk-in's Fog Tea while it bakes), serve;
(6) Odile asks for a comforting book: open the shelf, read the mood tags, pick The Lamplighter's Almanac
(the only book unlocked on Day 1, and it carries comforting); (7) read the patience bar and
the closing grade. Scripted orders live in the content file with ids so tests can check them.

## Content counts the validators enforce (task 02)

- Exactly 14 days with the table above; weather equals `day mod 3`; customers per day match the table;
  regular visits per day match the calendar; every regular has 5 visits on Days 1 to 13 and the Day 14 visit.
- Exactly 8 regulars with 5 scenes each (40), scene ids unique, scenes of one regular form one chain
  (scene k `next` is scene k+1, scene 5 has no `next`), every scene reachable from the regular's first
  visit under the friendship thresholds `[1, 2, 4, 6, 8]` and the calendar, no dangling scene, choice,
  option or guard references.
- 12 choices (regulars 2, 4, 6 and 8 have two, the others one); every option has a lean and 2 reaction
  beats; for every regular both endings are reachable by some choice combination; 16 ending blocks.
- Each scene has 6 to 9 beats; each beat at most 25 words; speakers are the regular, `you` or `narration`.
- Reading level: average Flesch-Kincaid grade of each scene at most 7.0 and no beat above grade 10, with
  a syllable-counting helper in the core; no word outside Latin-1 plus the font subset.
- Item tables: 6 drinks, 5 pastries, 5 books, 5 moods, 8 decor with the prices, steps, durations, tags and
  unlock days above; every book has two moods and every mood is on exactly two books; every drink and
  pastry step token is in the token lists.
- Every art slot id referenced by content exists in the 54-slot list in `assets-index.md`.
- Unlock schedule yields all 6 drinks, 5 pastries and 5 books by Day 11; each regular's favourites are
  valid ids.
