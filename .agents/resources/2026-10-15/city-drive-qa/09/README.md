# Task 09: AI traffic, QA evidence

Generated by the test suite (`SOAK_REPORT=1 BUDGET_REPORT=1 bun run city-drive:test`). Nothing here was
produced in a browser; the renderer was checked headlessly with Babylon `NullEngine`.

## soak-report.json

16 agents, 10 simulated minutes (36 000 fixed 1/60 s steps) on `district-1`, player parked on a
non-road tile, kinematic Rapier bodies and the city colliders installed. Four seeds. Each run is
checked every step.

| Check | Result (all 4 seeds) |
|---|---|
| Agent-minutes per run | about 159.7 (16 agents x 10 min, minus spawn ramp) |
| AI-AI collisions (OBB overlap) | 0 |
| Centre distance below 2.0 m | 0 (closest approach 5.6 m, i.e. about 1.4 m bumper to bumper) |
| Distance from the kinematic body to the lane polyline | at most 0.00001 m (limit 0.5 m) |
| Stopped, not explained by a stop line, the player or a stun | at most 1.5 s (limit 20 s) |
| Longest wait for a reservation | 8.4 s to 17.2 s |
| Least distance any agent covered in a 60 s window | 161 m (limit 20 m): no deadlock |
| Intersection edge entered without a reservation | 0 |
| Safety clamp at an unreserved stop line | 0 |
| Run time per 10-minute run | 5.7 s to 6.7 s (assert: at most 60 s; the original goal was 20 s) |

"Explained" means the stop is traced through the chain of leaders to a car waiting at a stop line for its
turn reservation (a reserved wait). Waits are bounded separately (assert: under 45 s).

Seeds 5 and 8 are kept as regression seeds. Before the exit-capacity check they deadlocked a road-end
U-turn for more than 370 s: cars inside the tee held the left-turn reservation while the U-turn was full of
cars waiting for the very turn the tee cars were holding. An agent now only reserves a turn when the
road after it has room (see `edgeCapacity` in `src/traffic/intersections.ts`).

## budget-report.json

The task 06/07 budget test now also parks 16 traffic cars in a 4 x 4 block filling the view ahead of the
player at each of the 9 poses (worst case: every car drawn). Hard budgets: 100 draw calls, 150 000
triangles; target below 60 draw calls.

| | Before (task 07 report) | With 16 traffic cars |
|---|---|---|
| Worst draw calls | 49 | 54 |
| Worst triangles | 125 267 | 140 995 |

Traffic costs 5 draw calls: one body source per model (4 models) plus one shared wheel source.
Cars are `InstancedMesh` per part. The Kenney wheel is 332 triangles (four per car, 64 in view); the
traffic cars use a shared 12-sided wheel of about 70 triangles with a light hub bar (so spin shows). With
the Kenney wheels the worst case was 158 403 triangles, over the hard budget.

## Notes

- Sweep (the informational grid in the budget test) also passes: worst 54 draws, 140 995 triangles.
- Determinism: the same seed plus the same scripted player input gives identical car and traffic hashes
  every second for 90 s; a different seed gives different traffic.
- Not run: any in-browser or on-device check (feel of the braking, whether hub-bar spin reads well, the
  car models in the dusk light). Those belong to the owner checkpoints.
