Generated by `bun run --filter @city-drive/core economy:report` (source:
`apps-games/city-drive-core/tool/economy-report.ts`, shared simulation in
`apps-games/city-drive-core/test/economy/sim.ts`) from the shipped content:

asserted by `test/economy/affordability.test.ts`.

the player buys the cheapest unowned cars they can afford. Replay bonuses are ignored (conservative).
