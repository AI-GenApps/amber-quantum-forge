# b1 round 2 review

Technical: icon 1024x1024 RGB opaque, key-art 1024x1536 RGB opaque, pump 1024x1024 RGBA (corners 0, outer 3% border alpha 0, bbox x108-958 y197-908 incl. shadow, about 83% of width incl. shadow, within tolerance). Contact sheets in this folder (a full size, b display size, c compare with accepted cottage, d pump on dark/paper/meadow/rock).

## pump: ACCEPTED
- Reads as one painter with house-cottage: same matte dry-brush, same soft ink shadow down-right, top-left light, same pitch.
- Round-1 faults fixed: wheel now centred, small and flush; squat iron-green drum wall visible under a low brass cap; no sheen streaks; four identical flanged stubs; only four small rivets.
- Clean on all four backgrounds, no fringe. At 32/48 px it is a dark drum with a bright brass top and four stubs: distinct silhouette. No text, no water.
- Minor, not blocking: iron wall has slightly blocky blotches and the brass cap is a flat disc more than a dome. Watch for these in later mechanical pieces (sluice, reservoir) so they match the cottage's softer brush.

## icon: REJECTED
- Palette: sampled lit ground about #6a853d, dark patches about #506a30 (lime/olive, not meadow #8aa468); water about #3b97ea (far brighter than #3b8dc4); roof about #df5a28 vs accepted cottage roof about #c66134. Overall far more saturated than the set.
- Ground: big dark saw-toothed blobs look like a camouflage stamp, not gouache.
- River is a single hump exiting both sides, not an S; the door step touches the water, so the focal point is cramped.
- Water highlight is a hard long white-blue streak: gloss.
- Good: cottage now about 46 percent of the width and matches accepted construction; no text; reads at 48 px.

## key-art: REJECTED
- Wrong view: a landscape with horizon hills and a fringed grassy ridge at y 27-33 percent (stippled edge), not an elevated table-top diorama; reads as a different set from the sprites.
- Colours too saturated (ground, water, roofs) against the bible.
- Button band is a huge empty field with repeating diagonal hatch strokes.
- No readable cast shadows on cottages, pump, sandbags, trees: objects look pasted.
- Pump shinier and smaller in detail than accepted pump; poplars are stubby tufted cones; willow too large; random dirt paths; far hedge mass at upper right is heavy.
- Good: title band calm and light, canal now branches logically, plain windmill sails, no text, no people.
