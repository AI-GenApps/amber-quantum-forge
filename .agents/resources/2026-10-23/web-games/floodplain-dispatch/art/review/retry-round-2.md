## key-art: REJECTED

Technical: 1024x1536 RGB opaque, PASS. Title band mean RGB(242,225,197), button band (246,231,206): about 9 RGB lighter at the bottom. Right-margin row-mean step across y1090..1200 is 5.0 RGB (brief limit: under 2), left margin 1.4. Borderline-visible warm drift, must be fixed (one continuous paper, no step).

Progress since round 1 (kept): the cluster is denser and has roof variety (2 terracotta cottages, slate townhouse, brown barn), the slab rim and cutbank are clean, sprites are the accepted pixels (no recolour, light top-left), water colour is right, grass now has calm tonal patches, poplars are tall and no longer saplings.

Reasons for rejection (rubric points 2, 3, 5, 6, 7):
1. Poplars merge into the barn roof (rubric 2, construction). The three poplars sit directly behind the barn so that their bases are cut by the barn ridge: they read as three cypress trees growing out of the barn roof. Their trunks/bases are never visible. They must stand on grass clear of the barn silhouette, with a visible base and ground shadow. Also three poplars in a tight row are identical clones, same shape, evenly spaced (stamped look).
2. River ends are frayed paintbrush tips (rubric 7, 3). Both ends of the river splay into ragged hair-like bristles (a dry-brush "broom" end, strongest at the right end x850-890,y960-1000 and left end x140-200,y990-1030). The brief asked for a soft irregular marsh pool fading into wet meadow, not bristles. This is the exact fringe/feather tell banned in the brief and does not match the accepted feature-graphic river ends (smooth lobed pools with a clean broken shoreline).
3. Fields are fringed stencils (rubric 3, 7). Both plots have a hard straight left edge and a torn, fringed right edge with stray pixels, and the furrows are ten-ish identical dark dashes in ruled horizontal rows that do not follow the slab pitch (they read as a stamped pattern or bacon strips). Plots are flat, with no lighter/darker ochre patches, and no wet-green margin where the feeder enters. The accepted feature-graphic fields have soft irregular edges, 4-5 long continuous slightly foreshortened furrows and a calm ochre base.
4. Canal feeders end at the plot edge with a fringed notch instead of running into the plot and tapering (rubric 2). The upper feeder runs into the field's right edge with a dark ragged cut; the lower feeder is a flat wedge that stops at the edge. The fork itself is a sharp acute "Y" with long straight tapered arms (like a wishbone), leaving an enclosed triangular green island between feeders and river; it is not the gentle curved bifurcation requested. The canal trunk also leaves the river in a one-sided hard hook at x410-440,y960.
5. Pitch is still a coaster (rubric 3). Slab about 840 wide by 650 deep (ratio about 0.77) with almost no taper, so it reads as a flat square tile with stickers on it, while the accepted feature-graphic is clearly foreshortened (about 0.5 to 0.55) with a front taper. Sprites (3/4 view) and terrain (near top-down) disagree. The brief asked for 884 x 600-650 and a slight taper; the delivered slab is narrower and square-fronted.
6. Surface and composition (rubric 6, 7). Grass is a uniform scribbly smudge texture with soft blotches, not the calm broad flat washes of the accepted grass (visible at 100%). The cluster is better but the three houses form a loose zig-zag with the pump and sandbags floating in an empty green middle (area around x470-620,y800-950 is a dead 250 px lawn); the pump is not related to the canal (it sits 60 px off the bank with nothing connecting it, and a gold cap that is a little hot next to the muted palette is acceptable).
7. Paper surround: bottom band is a different (lighter, more peach) wash from the title band and the right margin steps by 5 RGB across y1090-1200 (see Technical).

Verdict: REJECT. Accepted: none. Revision notes: retry/round-3/brief.md.
