# b1 round 1 review

Technical: all four files exist, correct sizes (icon 1024x1024 RGB, key-art 1024x1536 RGB, cottage/pump 1024x1024 RGBA, corners and 3% border alpha 0, bbox 81-82% incl. shadow). No accepted assets existed beforehand, so cottage is the anchor.

## house-cottage: ACCEPTED
Clean construction, matte gouache, readable at 32/48/96 px, terracotta roof reads at 32 px, top-right clear, soft ink shadow down-right, no fringe on meadow/paper/night/rock. Becomes the style anchor.

## pump: REJECTED
- Hand-wheel sits high on the dome, not at the exact centre as briefed.
- Dome is a large bulb covering the drum; not a squat drum with shallow dome; reads as a pipe valve/cross at 32 px.
- Smooth metallic gradients and sheen on brass/iron: 3D-render feel, drifts from the matte gouache of the cottage.
- Rivet micro-detail; slightly blotchy shadow.

## icon: REJECTED
- Ground is saturated yellow-green with a repeating lozenge brush-stamp pattern: AI tell, off-palette (not #8aa468).
- Cottage too small (~33% width); river dominates focal hierarchy; river is a mild wave, not a bold S.
- Water has many streaks; roof/water do read at 48 px, but composition is weak for an award-level icon.

## key-art: REJECTED
- Stippled/pointillist micro-detail and frayed dotted ground edge: different technique from the accepted cottage.
- Diorama is a small floating island with large empty paper top and bottom; the button band lacks the briefed meadow-and-paper surround; weak cover composition.
- Pump unreadable, cypress-like trees instead of poplars, lattice windmill sails, illogical canal branching.
- Title band itself is calm and light (pass), no text/people/UI (pass).
