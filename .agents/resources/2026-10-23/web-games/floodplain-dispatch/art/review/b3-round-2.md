# Review: batch b3, round 2

Technical: all five files open cleanly; backdrops are 1024x1536 RGB (opaque), feature-graphic is 1536x1024 RGB (opaque). Centre-zone (x 6-94 percent, y 14-74 percent) luminance std: valley 1.06, delta 0.98, highland 0.96, estuary 0.52 percent of mean. No text, no objects in any centre. Contact sheets in this folder: a-full-1/2, b-display-board/plain, c-edge-tops/bottoms, c-consistency, z-* detail crops, feature-full, feature-store-crop.

Shared caveat (all four backdrops): the designer delivered a flat calm field with scenery only as corner fragments at the top and bottom, no left/right-edge scenery, and no hedge ring. That is thinner than the round-2 notes asked for. It is accepted for valley and highland because the fragments are clean, the centre is excellent and each region still reads at 360 px; the delta and estuary have specific faults below.

## backdrop-valley - ACCEPTED
- Clean matte gouache, same crisp-edged shape language and top-left light as the accepted icon/cottage; hedge masses, pale track, muted ochre pasture strip (#cba14a, within the ochre token) and four tiny poplars with short down-right shadows all read at 360 px.
- Centre is plain meadow (#85a560 region), no seam, no vignette, no frame. No text, no water, no blue.
- Minor (not blocking): the corner scenery sits partly inside the HUD/tray bands and there is no side-edge scenery; acceptable because the board and tray cover the centre-band area anyway.

## backdrop-highland - ACCEPTED
- Dry-stone walls with soft down-right shade, boulders, scrub and a goat track read instantly as dry terraced hillside; palette is sage/ochre/rock/slate, no water, no snow. Centre calm (#9ea071), the blotches from round 1 are gone.
- Minor (not blocking): grass-blade strokes and small stone detail are slightly finer and softer than the valley but still inside the same hand; keep an eye on this in later sets.

## backdrop-delta - REJECTED
- Silt islands and reed dabs are saturated yellow-orange (about #d6b252, saturation 0.61 vs the bible's muted silt ochre #b9a062 at 0.46). They are the loudest colour in the whole set, which the round-1 notes forbade ("no saturated yellow wedges"), and they glow against the olive ground (generic AI over-saturation tell).
- The bottom-left braid starts at about y 1170 (76 percent), i.e. inside the tray band, at high contrast; the top-right braid also sits across the HUD band. Calm-band rule broken.
- Positives to keep: braided green-grey channels (not blue), islands with reeds, olive centre, clear difference from valley.

## backdrop-estuary - REJECTED
- A blue-grey tidal creek is painted at top-left (y 0-175) in the same colour as the sea, so there are two water bodies and it sits in the HUD band; the brief allowed muted sea at ONE edge only and creeks as mud curls.
- The shoreline and sand tongues climb to about y 1290 (84 percent) with strong contrast, inside the tray band, instead of staying in the bottom 8-10 percent.
- Value is out of family: mean luminance about 181 versus about 135-143 for the other three; paper-light tray panels and the board's sand cells would not separate from it.
- Grass tufts are thin pencil-like hatching and the sea/creek have soft wet-bleed edges, drifting from the crisp gouache of valley/delta.
- Positives to keep: wavering coast with wet-sand band, no foam, muted sea (#77949a), calm flat centre (std 0.5 percent).

## feature-graphic - ACCEPTED
- Reads as the same world as the icon and accepted sprites: thin tabletop slab on warm paper surround, flat brown cutbank, top-left light, matte gouache. All sprites are the accepted ones composited uniformly with their baked shadows; no fringe. The barn is a no-hay variant of the accepted barn (as requested in the brief).
- Water logic is fixed: one river, a visibly dug canal with bank lips branching at a rounded junction, passing the sluice gate (water continues through the piers), pump standing on dry bank beside the canal with its stub touching the bank, canal ending in a ragged tapered field end. River colour #348cc7 matches the game water family and is no longer neon. Left end is an inset cove tapering into grass; right end is a soft pool before the slab edge. Fields have furrow strokes and ragged edges. No text, people, animals, boats, smoke or UI.
- Composition: focal pump/canal/gate at about x 52 percent, y 50 percent; all content inside the 1024x500 crop (slab spans y 175-850 of 1024).
- Minor (not blocking): the two poplars and the townhouse are under-scaled against the cottages (the poplar should be the tallest tree), and the grass mottling is a little busy beside the flatter icon grass. The next set of sprites/scenes should keep scale rules in mind.
