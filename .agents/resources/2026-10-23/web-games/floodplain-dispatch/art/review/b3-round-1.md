# Review b3 round 1 - backdrop-valley, backdrop-delta, backdrop-highland, backdrop-estuary, feature-graphic

Technical: all five files present, valid PNG, RGB opaque (no alpha), exact sizes (backdrops 1024x1536, feature 1536x1024). Centre-zone luminance variation is within spec (std about 2 to 3 percent). No text found. Water-blue check: estuary sea strip is muted (about #97adb9), fine. Contact sheets: a-full.png, b-display-plain.png, b-display-board.png (board/tray overlay), edge-corners.png, z-*.png and feature-store-crop.png / feature-360.png.

Note: the accepted set contains no key-art (b1 key-art was rejected in round 4), so the feature graphic was compared against accepted icon, pump, houses, trees and the b1 round-4 key-art rejection reasons.

## backdrop-valley - REJECTED
- [6, 7] The decorative edge is squeezed into a hard rectangular rim of about 50 px that stops abruptly at a straight inner line, so the whole image reads as a flat green rectangle with a framed, vignette-like border (brief: no frame, no ring, fades smoothly). The motifs look like paint smears, not scenery.
- [2, 7] The left and right edge strips show vertical stretched streaks (resampling artefact from the piecewise column remap); teal-grey smudges read as dirt, not hedgerows or poplars.
- [3] Ochre wedges in the corners are a hot, saturated yellow (about #e0b030) outside the muted ochre family and brighter than anything on the accepted sprites.
- [8] Identity is weak: no readable hedge, strip, track or poplar dots at 360 px; it is just green.

## backdrop-delta - REJECTED
- [6, 7] Same rectangular, boxed rim as valley: a lighter, hazier band follows a rounded rectangle just inside the edges (picture-frame look). The braided silt ribbons, reed dabs and islands exist only in a few corner scraps that are invisible at display size.
- [8] At 360 px it is barely distinguishable from the valley (olive green, plain). The delta needs visible silt-ribbon and channel character in the margins.
- [7] Muddy low-contrast olive wash with grey haze; no pigment structure.

## backdrop-highland - REJECTED
- [7] The whole surface is mottled with blotchy yellow-ochre clouds across the board zone (centre included), which reads as stains or camouflage, not dry ground; the brief asked for broad flat ground with only a few paler worn patches. The blotches also create uneven luminance behind the board.
- [8] No readable terraces, dry-stone walls, boulders or goat track at display size: the stone motifs are a few tiny dashes at the extreme top and bottom edge.
- [6, 7] Same rectangular rim/feathered inner box as the other three; grey-green-ochre combination is muddy and desaturated into sickly tones, not sage, ochre and slate.

## backdrop-estuary - REJECTED (closest to acceptable)
- [6, 7] The sea is a hard, straight, full-height blue stripe along the left edge with a wobbly vertical contour; it reads as a ruler or UI stripe, not as a tidal edge. No shoreline softness, ripple or sand-to-sea transition, and the stripe is flat.
- [6, 7] Same boxed rim: tidal creek curls and salt-grass tufts sit in a thin border and stop at a straight inner edge. Pale sand surface with soft pale swirl marks across the entire board zone looks like marble or wet plaster rather than gouache sand.
- [8] Tidal identity (creeks, grass, ripples) is not readable at 360 px.

## feature-graphic - REJECTED
- [2] The river is a flat ribbon that is sheared off with straight vertical cuts at both the left and right edges of the slab and floats over the cutbank with no drop or exit. This is the same construction fault as the rejected b1 key-art.
- [2] The canal starts as a square-ended strip against an ochre field and runs through the sluice, then disappears behind the pump and re-emerges below it; it joins the river through an odd notch in the bank. Water logic is unreadable (does the pump lift water from the river to the field, or does the field drain to the river?), and the canal is a pasted strip, not a dug channel.
- [3, 7] River water is about #2f91d7, brighter and more saturated than the game's water #3b8dc4 and the accepted icon; it is the hottest element and dominates the composition. The orange fields are flat feathered rectangles with no row structure, and the pump brass reads as saturated gold rather than muted brass #b08a3c. A stray yellow hay block sits at the barn.
- [3] Mixed pitch: sprites are pasted at their low 3/4 angle onto a terrain that is seen from higher up, with a thick toy slab and a pebbly brown cutbank unlike the rest of the painting. Reads as a collage of sprites, not one painted diorama.
- [8] The brief asked for a river just beginning to swell and a canal and pump feeding fields and houses; the story is not told (river width is uniform and unremarkable, nothing is being fed). The pump is oversized and visually heavy at the focal point.
- What is good and must be kept: slab-on-paper-table concept, warm paper surround with generous margins, accepted sprites faithfully reproduced (windmill, cottages, townhouse, barn, reservoir on a mound, sandbags, willow), no text/people/boats/smoke, all key content inside the 1024x500 centre crop.
