## key-art: REJECTED

Technical (pass): 1024x1536, opaque RGB PNG, no alpha. Title band mean RGB(242,225,199), button band (243,226,201). Side-margin row-mean step across y1090..1200 is 1.76 RGB (<2). Bands empty. No text, UI, people, animals, boats found. Sprites are the accepted files (verified visually: no recolour, light top-left, shadows down-right). Slab rim, cutbank (thin, flat two-plane brown), and water colour are clean and acceptable. Grass saturation matches the accepted feature-graphic.

Reasons for rejection (rubric points 2, 3, 6, 7, 8):
1. Reads as a sparse placeholder map, not a featured cover. Only two cottages, scattered far apart on a huge empty meadow (the whole right third and the upper middle are dead grass). No cluster, no sense of "a town to protect", no roof-colour variety. The accepted feature-graphic has a dense, deliberate cluster plus barn, townhouse, reservoir and gate; this has none of that rhythm. Focal point (cottage + pump + canal) is not a focal point: the cottages are 250 px away from the pump and the eye has nowhere to land. (Rubric 6, 8.)
2. Poplars are wrong in scale and read as two tiny cypress saplings floating at the right edge (about 64 px tall next to 133 px tall cottages; poplar should be the tallest tree, taller than the willow and well above a cottage ridge). At 360 px they are two specks. Willow is large, poplars tiny: inconsistent scale rules between siblings. (Rubric 3, 4.)
3. Water logic and shape. Both river ends are fat bulbous "sausage / tadpole head" lobes (right end has a swirl highlight like a balloon) rather than soft marsh pools; the river as a whole is a single wavy sausage with identical width from end to end. The canal fork is a sharp pinched Y with a pooled blob at the node (about x440,y830) and the lower feeder is a thin wedge that looks like a different brush from the trunk; the canal mouth at the river ends in a pointed land wedge. The feeders touch the pale edge of the field rectangles without entering them. (Rubric 2, 7.)
4. Fields are two flat rectangular stickers with frayed left edges and five ruled parallel strokes each, edge-on to a top-down plate; they do not sit in the terrain like the accepted feature-graphic fields (which have soft irregular tonal patches around them and foreshortened rows). (Rubric 3.)
5. Pitch is not convincingly shared: the slab is a near-square tile (about 864 x 690, depth/width 0.8) seen almost top-down with a hairline cutbank, while sprites are at 3/4. The accepted feature-graphic slab is clearly foreshortened (depth/width about 0.5) with a visible front taper. At 360 px the slab reads as a flat coaster with stickers on it. (Rubric 3.)
6. Plate surface is a uniform scribbly mid-green with no calm tonal patches (no sage, no meadow-deep zones, no pale dry patches), unlike the accepted grass. Edges darken towards the slab rim like a soft vignette. (Rubric 7.)

Verdict: REJECT (missing nothing, but fails style/composition/purpose). Accepted: none. Revision notes are in retry/round-2/brief.md.
