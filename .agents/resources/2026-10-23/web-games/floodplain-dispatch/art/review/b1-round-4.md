# Review b1 round 4 (final) - assets: icon, key-art

Technical: icon 1024x1024 RGB opaque, valid; key-art 1024x1536 RGB opaque, valid. No text, borders or vignettes on either. Manifest valid. Contact sheets: a-full.png, b-icon-display.png, b-keyart-360.png, c-compare-icon.png, c-keyart-*.png, z1-z4 crops.

## icon - ACCEPTED
- Round-3 faults fixed: cottage now about 56 percent of canvas width and the river a clear second mass (about 13 percent of height); shoreline is now a thin, softened, broken ink-green line that sits with the painted cottage contour (no clip-art stroke); water has matte brush direction and one short light streak; dry meadow strip kept between step and bank.
- Style: cottage matches accepted/house-cottage.png (roof, chimney, door, step, windows, short soft down-right shadow, top-left light); palette ground ~#8aa468, water ~#3b8dc4, roof ~#c55f33. Same gouache hand as the cottage and pump anchors.
- Readability: at 48 and 96 px (square, circle, rounded-square masks) it reads as orange-roofed house over blue water on green; silhouette and value structure hold; both essentials stay inside the circular mask.
- Remaining minor weaknesses (not disqualifying): the river is a gentle double wave rather than a strongly pronounced S; the two dark shade smudges (top-left, right) are slightly dirty-looking; the composition is plain. Accepted as a clean, honest, on-style icon; no rubric point fails.

## key-art - REJECTED
Fixed since round 3: the horizontal seam is gone (surround is one continuous paper surface, title mean ~#eadec1, no band edge); model now reads as a thick tabletop slab with a cutbank and soft shadow; scale increased; poplars are smooth flames; willow pulled in from the edge; canal fields taper.
Reasons for rejection (rubric points in brackets):
- [3, 7] Windmill hub still carries an orange-red diamond cap right next to the flood-alert colour; the brief explicitly required weathered timber only, and said no orange.
- [3, 7] Crop fields are a hot saturated yellow-gold (~#f5b820) with ruled stripes, well outside the ochre (#c8923a) family and the muted gouache palette; the pump brass is likewise a saturated gold rather than the accepted muted brass #b08a3c, so the pump does not match accepted/pump.png.
- [2] Water construction errors: the river is a flat ribbon that is sheared off at the terrain edge at upper right and at lower left (floats over the cutbank lip with no logical exit or drop); the canal is a pasted blue strip with a square cut end laid on the river bank rather than branching from it, and it passes through the pump flange.
- [3, 7] Rim of the terrain is still frayed and feathered along the top and sides, which the brief asked to remove; the cutbank is a pebbly brown texture unlike the rest of the painting; the willow reads as a palm or cabbage crown rather than a drooping willow.
- [3] Mixed pitch: cottages sit at the low 3/4 angle of the accepted sprite while the pump and terrain are seen from noticeably higher up.
This was the last round. The key-art slot remains for a placeholder or later work; nothing in this file is accepted.
