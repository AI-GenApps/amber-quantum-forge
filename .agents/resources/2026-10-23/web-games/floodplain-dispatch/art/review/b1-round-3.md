## icon - REJECTED
Fixed since round 2: palette, ground wash, S meander, strip between door step and water, cottage matches accepted, soft down-right shadow, no text.
Reasons for rejection:
- Rubric 3 (style consistency): both river banks are a perfectly uniform near-black (#1d201b) vector stroke, about 4-5 px, clean as clip-art; the accepted cottage and pump have painterly broken contours. The water reads as a vector shape pasted on gouache.
- Rubric 4 (readability at 48 px): cottage is about 20 px wide at 48 px, the river a thin wave; about 60 percent of the canvas is empty flat green. Focal point is weak; it does not look like a featured-quality store icon. In a circular mask only part of the river survives.
- Rubric 6/8: river is a gentle wave rather than a clear S; composition has dead space top-left.
Revision notes are in round-4/brief.md.

## key-art - REJECTED
Fixed since round 2: palette, tabletop-ish framing, cast shadows, pump matches accepted, willow reduced, one path, calm title band (mean #eeddbe).
Reasons for rejection:
- Rubric 5/6 (technical/composition): a hard full-width horizontal seam at y about 1100-1190 where the paper surround switches to a different greenish wash; reads as two stitched panels, and the model's front edge is cut by it. Button band is not a continuous surface.
- Rubric 3/7: diorama is a flat rounded-rectangle sticker with a frayed stippled rim, seen almost straight down, not an elevated table-top model; it fills only about 54 percent of the width, a postage stamp in a sea of paper. Grass has a repeated scalloped dab pattern.
- Rubric 2/7: poplars are cypress-like with a fish-scale leaf pattern; windmill sails/hub use a saturated orange-red close to the flood-alert colour; willow crown touches the terrain edge; canal T-branches are straight stick shapes.
Revision notes are in round-4/brief.md.
