# Review b2 round 3 (reviewer verdict)

## item-rainy-city-5 (Black Rubber Boots): REJECTED

Technical: PASS. 1024x1024 RGBA, corners and outer 4 percent border alpha 0, pair bbox 66.7 x 61.9 percent (within 78 x 62), centred, top-right empty, no halo on linen or night, loop holes transparent.

Rubric failures:
- 2 / 8 (construction, purpose): does NOT read as a pair of boots. Each shape is a bulbous domed toe on a long flaring tube with a wide open mouth and a tiny hanging loop. At 64 and 96 px it reads as two bells, thermos flasks or cartridge cases. The silhouette still has the pinched keyhole waist under the toe dome (the exact defect the round 2 notes forbade), then flares towards the cuff (a bell shape, not a parallel-sided wellington). There is no ankle/vamp/foot logic and no sole, so the toe-up lean is not legible; the top dome looks like a cap on a pipe. The horizontal curved band across the dome splits it like a hat, and the dark rectangular shade step at mid-height (a stepped notch on the right side of each boot) makes the form look like a stacked, melting construction.
- 3 / 7 (style, AI tells): rendering is smooth glossy vector/3D-ish: curved specular streaks, soft convex shading on the dome, uniform clean contour, no visible gouache brush direction or paper tooth. Against the accepted espadrilles, tee, trousers and trench (visible marker pressure, stepped flat planes, dry-brush edge) this looks like a different artist and technique. The contour is a brown-outline-on-brown-fill with reddish edge, not the warm ink marker of the set.
- 4 (readability): mush at 64 to 96 px; only a dark blob pair with a ring, no distinct footwear features.
- Spec: the lifted plane `#5b514c` is a wide brown band that makes the boot look brown-grey translucent rather than black rubber; the sole line `#161210` is missing (the brief asked for a visible low sole line like item-riviera-5). The pull loop is on the outside of the rim, hanging like a bell clapper, rather than attached to the back top of the shaft.
- Accepted siblings checked: item-riviera-5, item-riviera-1, item-rainy-city-4, item-rainy-city-2 (contact sheet c).

Verdict: reject; re-brief for round 4 (see ../round-4/brief.md).
