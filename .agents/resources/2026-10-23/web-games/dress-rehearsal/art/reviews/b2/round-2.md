## item-riviera-4 (Orange Wrap Skirt): ACCEPTED
- Round-1 problem (thin reddish contour) is fixed: warm ink contour about 7-8 px around silhouette, wrap edge and bow; 3-4 px hem stitch and waistband seam. Matches item-riviera-1/3 weight.
- Reads instantly as tangerine orange (not red or yellow), A-line wrap with diagonal overlap, tied bow, gathers. Width 54.7 percent (limit 55), centred, shorter than 88 percent as the brief allows.
- Clean at 64/96/128 px; no halo on linen or night. No text. Slight streaky gouache grain is within the accepted pieces' range.

## item-rainy-city-3 (Grey Wool Jumper): ACCEPTED
- Round-1 problems fixed: stains gone, quiet mid-grey torso with a clean light plane top-left and a few crisp deep planes lower-right and under the sleeves; sleeves are straight tubes at about 25 degrees with cuffs the same width as the sleeve ends; dropped-shoulder seams.
- Ribbed collar, cuffs and hem have 8-10 broad lines, V-stitch ring is sparse and evenly spaced, no text-like marks. Neutral warm grey, contour 7 px ink. Width 79.7 percent (limit 80), centred.
- Reads as a knit top at 96 px and sits well beside the accepted tee and trench. Slightly plain, but consistent.

## item-rainy-city-5 (Black Rubber Boots): REJECTED
- Purpose (rubric 8): does not read as rubber boots. The silhouette is a peanut or hourglass (bulbous toe, pinched waist, flared bottom); at 64 and 96 px it reads as two black slippers or clogs, and beside the espadrilles it does not look like a tall pull-on boot.
- Construction (rubric 2): the shaft opening shows a dark sphere-like blob inside a ring, not a cylinder opening; the pull tab is a square block that reads as a cube or bead hanging off the rim, not a loop strap; the horizontal toe seam cuts the boot into two halves.
- Shading and readability (rubric 4, 7): body is a near-uniform dark mass; the contour is nearly the same value as the body, so the form reads flat. Highlights are acceptable in size but give a slightly glossy look.
- Good and to keep: toes up and openings down (matches item-riviera-5 camera), mirror pair, clean gap, 65 percent by 62 percent, flat calm black, real alpha.
