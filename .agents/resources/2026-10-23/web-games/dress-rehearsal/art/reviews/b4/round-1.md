## ACCEPTED
- item-mountain-2 (cream cable-knit): cream clearly warmer than white, 3 body cables + 1 per sleeve, roll neck and ribbed hem/cuffs consistent, symmetric, same ink weight as riviera-1. Reads at 64 px.
- item-mountain-3 (tan hiking trousers): tan correct, knee seams, zip pockets, cord locks, button all logical; legs symmetric with a clear gap; 87.7 percent height within limit.
- item-mountain-4 (orange puffer): 6 body baffles, 5 per sleeve, stand collar, central zip, zipped pockets, elastic cuffs; reads orange at 64 px. Saturation is at the tag value; accepted.
- item-mountain-5 (black trail boots): mirrored pair, parallel, toes up, 4 eyelet pairs, toe-cap seam, lug sole with 5 bumps per side, padded collar; lifted planes not gloss; clearly distinct from the platform boots.
- item-mountain-6 (blue canvas rucksack): two straps with brass buckles, flap pocket, leather base, carry loop; canvas streaks fit the material rules; cobalt reads blue.
- item-mountain-7 (grey wool beanie): rib lines converge logically, cuff ribs finer, neutral grey, distinct from the grey jumper.
- item-studio-1 (violet origami top): faceted planes logical, asymmetric wing shoulder, diagonal hem, light facets top-left; reads as a sculptural top and violet.
- item-studio-2 (grey pleated culottes): equal pleat fans on both legs, waistband, clear leg gap; neutral grey; shorter than 88 percent as intended. Reads as pleated wide-leg at 96 px.
- item-studio-4 (cobalt blazer-coat): notch lapels, dropped shoulders, one covered button, welt pockets, cuffs, symmetric sleeves; clearly cobalt and different from the navy opera coat. Minor: button is not the darker shade; invisible at display size.
- item-studio-5 (black platform boots): thick two-tone sole, block heel, mirrored pair, no tread; reads as platform and differs from trail boots.

## REJECTED
- item-mountain-8 (green wool scarf): reads as a stiff flat green bar or wrapped sachet, not a soft scarf; no rib marks, layers only peek out one side, fringe drawn as fat tubes and crowded at one end, tiny and dull at 64 px (rubric 4, 7, 8).
- item-studio-3 (white drape dress): drawn as worn on an invisible body (bust, waist, hips), violating the flat-lay camera rule and not matching the accepted flat gala dress (rubric 3, 2); colour reads cream (median f8eeda) and is too close to the cream sweater (rubric 8); soft airbrushed shading instead of stepped planes; right-side contour thins; narrow 43 percent width.
