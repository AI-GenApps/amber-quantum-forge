## item-mountain-8 - ACCEPTED
- Reads as a plush forest-green wool scarf folded in thirds (loose Z-fold), diagonal lower-left to upper-right; three stacked layers visible, rounded fold ends, wavy edges, vertical rib marks, two deep bands near each end, soft fold shadow. Fixes every round-1 complaint (no stiff bar, no fat sausage fringe).
- Fringe: 7 thin tapered strands at each end, same character and spacing, clear transparent gaps.
- Colour median ~#3d6a4a, lively forest green, not teal or olive. Contour weight and knit treatment sit with item-mountain-7 and item-mountain-2; reads as a scarf and green at 64/96/128 on linen and night. No text, halo, glints; top-right empty.
- Minor: fringe tips are slightly pod-like, invisible at display size.

## item-studio-3 - REJECTED
- Shading is soft, smudgy airbrushed pencil-style blending, not 2-3 flat stepped gouache planes with crisp edges (explicit round-2 request, not applied). Looks like a different artist next to item-riviera-1, item-mountain-2 and item-gala-2 (rubric 3, 7).
- Contour inconsistent: heavy on the left flank and hem, hairline on the right flank, ruffle edge, bodice top and inner folds; brief required a continuous 7-10 px contour.
- Still reads as a gown on an invisible body: waist seam, strapless inner bodice panel with a floating top edge behind the drape, gravity-hung flared skirt (rubric 2, round-2 brief asked for a flat-laid garment).
- Colour is a greyish ivory (median ~#eee8de) with dirty grey shadow smears rather than clean #f8f4ea with flat #d8d0c0/#b5ab98 shade planes.
- Positives (kept for round 3): width 61 percent fills the box, asymmetric one-shoulder concept, single diagonal wrap, longer left hem, good silhouette at 64-128 px, clean alpha.
