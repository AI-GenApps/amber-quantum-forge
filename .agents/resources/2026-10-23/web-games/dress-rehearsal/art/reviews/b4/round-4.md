# Review: batch b4, round 4 (final) - Dress Rehearsal

## item-studio-3 - REJECTED

Technical (pass): 1024x1024 RGBA, corners alpha 0, outer 4% border alpha 0, physical bbox about 61.8 x 82.7 percent, top-right empty, no checkerboard, no halo seen on linen, night or grey (sheet-a-full.png).

Reasons for rejection:
1. Rubric 3 (style consistency): beside accepted item-riviera-1, item-gala-2 and the cream sweater (sheet-c-light.png, sheet-c-dark.png, sheet-c-128.png) it still looks like a clean vector sticker. The fills are near-flat smooth off-white with almost no visible dry-brush or paper tooth, and the contour is a thin, near-uniform dark line on the long straight runs (left flank, right flank, hem), lighter than the accepted pieces' hand-pressed marker line. The accepted set has richer painted modelling and tactile texture; this piece is visibly from a different, flatter hand.
2. Rubric 2 (construction): the garment is a narrow bodice on a plain triangle. The skirt is a single wedge bounded by two ruler-straight edges and a ruler-straight hem, so it reads as a paper cone or napkin, not a dress with a flared skirt. The right shoulder ends in a hard stubby corner with a sharp vertical drop, and the waist notch exists on one side only. It is not a believable flat-laid garment.
3. Rubric 2/8 (the single twist): the wrap band is a pale translucent-looking grey sash with a double line at the shoulder. It does not read as one clear fold of cloth wrapping from the right hip to the left shoulder. The three skirt "ribbons" are long uniform-width S-curved stripes with a darker edge. They read as decorative stripes or painted bands, not drape folds, and they do not visibly originate from the band.
4. Rubric 7 (AI tells): the grey ribbon fills have soft, slightly airbrushed tones, which the brief explicitly asked to remove. The overall look is generic and smooth.
5. Rubric 4: at 48 and 96 px (sheet-b-display.png) it reads as a white dress, but the folds blur into grey stripes and the outline is thinner than the neighbours at 128 px.

What passed: warm white colour tag, asymmetric hem (about 15 percent drop, lowest at bottom-left), no text, no body, correct centring, valid alpha.

Verdict: REJECT. This is the final round of the batch, so the slot stays open for a placeholder. Nothing copied to accepted/ and accepted/manifest.json is unchanged.
