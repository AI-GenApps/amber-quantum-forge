## item-studio-3: REJECTED

Technical (pass): 1024x1024 RGBA, all four corners alpha 0, outer 4 percent border alpha 0, solid bbox about 611x938 (about 60 x 92 percent, centred), top-right corner empty, no checkerboard, no halo on linen or night, shadow faint. Silhouette reads as a white one-shoulder dress at 48/96/128 px.

Reasons for rejection:
1. Style drift against the accepted set (rubric 3). The accepted pieces (item-riviera-1, item-gala-2, item-mountain-2) have a thin, hand-wobbled contour (measured about 6 px, pressure-varied) and visible brush/paper texture inside the fills. This dress has a perfectly uniform 9 to 10 px contour (measured 9, 9, 10 at three rows) with no pressure variation and clean vector-smooth fills with almost no brush grain. Side by side (c_compare.png) it looks like a sticker/vector icon from another artist, noticeably bolder-outlined than every sibling.
2. Generic AI tell (rubric 7). The folds are smooth, slightly feathered, airbrush-like slivers; near the right flank (e_zoom_fold.png) there are faint translucent pale slivers and soft gradient edges rather than crisp stepped gouache planes. The shading steps are weaker and more "digital gradient" than item-gala-2 or the origami top.
3. Construction/purpose (rubric 2, 8). The "single twisted fold from right hip to left shoulder" does not read as one logical wrap: the fold planes radiate from one point at the right hip like leaf petals or a starburst, with a separate swoosh at the bust, so it reads as a generic gathered one-shoulder dress rather than a drape. The hem is only weakly asymmetric: the lowest point is mid-hem, and the left corner is only about 20 px lower than the right, so "longer on the left" is not clear. The silhouette still carries a worn-body shaped waist and flared skirt.
4. Palette: the white is clean and warm (good) and distinct from item-mountain-2; shade and deep tones are acceptable. This is not a reason for rejection.

Verdict: reject; see b4/round-4/brief.md.
