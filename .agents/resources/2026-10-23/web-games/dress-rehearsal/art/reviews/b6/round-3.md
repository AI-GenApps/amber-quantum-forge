# Review b6 round 3

## backdrop-vintage - ACCEPTED
- Technical: 1024x1536 RGB, no alpha, opaque, no border.
- Calm core (x 14-86, y 12-88): mean about #e0cebb, range #d2bea5-#ebdbc8, only weave and grain, no props. Matches accepted backdrop-gala/mountain/riviera in grain, weave, softness and wash width (checked side by side and at 48 px).
- Ric-rac: now ONE continuous even zig-zag, fully inside the frame, no ghost remains. Its darkest tone (about #a55642) equals the accepted backdrop-gala ribbon (#a63739), so prop strength is consistent.
- Vinyl corner: now small (about 8 percent of width, 10 percent of height), pale warm grey-brown, blank pale label, faint grooves, no gloss, no dark rim, inside the right prop ring. It is very quiet; slightly flat and cool-grey compared with the painted props, but it is no longer the focal point and is invisible as a distraction at display size. Acceptable.
- Left/right balance OK (ric-rac + daisy vs buttons + vinyl). Rust wash identifiable at 48 px.

## feature-graphic - REJECTED
- Technical OK: 1536x1024 RGB, opaque, essential content inside y 137-887 (boots end about y 880).
- Gown, heels, clutch, pearls, trench, trousers, ribbon, swatches and umbrella are fine; the umbrella is now correctly on the lower-left to upper-right diagonal with flat wood tones and matches item-rainy-city-8.
- FAIL (rubric 7 / brief note 3): the boots are plastic-gloss rendered. Each boot has soft rounded specular streaks on the toe cap and on the shaft with a gradient fall-off, and a smooth airbrushed black body. The accepted pieces (trousers, trench, umbrella, heels) use flat stepped planes with no soft glints. The boots look like a different, 3D-rendered style next to the rest of the board.
- FAIL (rubric 2/3): the boots read as chunky lug-sole hiking or work boots, not rubber wellingtons: a thick tread-lugged sole rim around toe and shaft, a very short shaft, and the dark collar opening at the bottom of the boot is a plain oval with a rim. The ink contour is also thinner and softer than the 7-10 px contour on the other pieces.
- Boot pair is small and heavy relative to the heels (visually much darker mass at the bottom of the right look), weakening balance with the gold heels.
