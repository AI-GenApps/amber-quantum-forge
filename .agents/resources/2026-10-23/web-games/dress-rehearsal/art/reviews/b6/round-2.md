## Clients

Eyes equal with highlights, ears and earrings symmetrical, mouths closed, no glasses, no hands, no text (violet pin is a plain pentagon). At 180x240 the six are clearly distinct (hair, age, outfit colour: sky shirt, olive jacket, black stand collar, green fleece, charcoal coat, mustard cardigan). No halo, no fringe, no cast shadow.
