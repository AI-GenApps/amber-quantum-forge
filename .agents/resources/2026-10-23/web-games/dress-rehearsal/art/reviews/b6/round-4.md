1. Gloss still present (rubric 7, brief must-not "no plastic gloss"). At 4x the toe caps carry a pale diagonal highlight streak and the left shaft edge has a soft, tapered, semi-transparent lighter band that fades out, not a flat hard-edged shape. Reads as shiny vinyl, unlike the matte stepped gouache of the heels and umbrella. At the 1024x500 store crop the boots still look glossy and smooth.
2. Shape (rubric 2/8). The shafts still have an hourglass waist (wide toe, pinched ankle, flared shaft) rather than a long straight wellington shaft; they read as tubes or socks/pipes, not rubber boots. The toe seam is a thin line with no sole edge visible, so they lack the footwear cue.
3. Line/style (rubric 3). Contour is thinner and softer than the heavy warm ink on the trousers and umbrella; the boots are the weakest, most airbrushed objects in the sheet and do not look like the same artist.
4. Placement is fine (centred under trousers, bottoms at y 869, inside the safe band) but does not offset the above.

This is the final round, so the asset stays rejected and the slot should use a placeholder or be rebuilt later. No brief written.
