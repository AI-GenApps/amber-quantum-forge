# Review: retry batch round 1 (Dress Rehearsal)

Method: PIL technical checks, contact sheets in this folder (a_keyart, a_feature, b_display_items, c_items_vs_accepted, c_backdrops_vs_accepted, c_studio_props, d_transparent_dark_light, z_* zooms), every image and sheet viewed.

Technical: all five files exist, correct size and mode (RGBA 1024 for the two sprites with alpha 0 corners and border; RGB for key-art 1024x1536, backdrop 1024x1536, feature 1536x1024). Sprites: boots bbox 65.7 x 61.4 percent, dress 61.8 x 88.6 percent, both inside the scale table.

## backdrop-studio: ACCEPTED
- Calm core is pixel-identical to the accepted gala core (mean 231/213/191, std 2.2), so paper and weave match the five siblings.
- Violet glaze (edge about 209,182,197) reads clearly violet at 48 px beside the siblings, fades to linen by x 12-14 percent, no yellow halo band.
- Four props (cream dotted pattern offcut, violet spool, chalk wedge with trail, violet ribbon curl), all inside the ring, uncropped, soft, no ink contour, no text or numerals. Sizes are at the small end of the brief (below the 95 px target) but equal to or close to the accepted sibling props at display size, and they are not in the calm core. Chalk reads as a pale lavender wedge, ribbon is small: minor, not noticeable at board size.
- No gloss, sparkle, vignette or border. Consistent with siblings. Accepted.

## item-rainy-city-5: REJECTED
- Silhouette: each boot is still a straight cylinder with a flat open mouth at the bottom. Beside item-studio-5 and item-riviera-5 at 48 and 96 px it reads as two cups or tubes, not boots. Same failure as the earlier four rounds; the toe box has no form and no instep.
- Gloss: a hard lightning-bolt shaped glare on each shaft, a rounded pale sheen read, plus taupe-grey toe caps that are much lighter than the #5b514c lifted plane. This is plastic/gloss and an AI tell.
- Contour is a uniform smooth vector-like line, thicker and cleaner than item-studio-5; fills are smooth gradient-like dark with little paper tooth.
- Pull tab sits below the cuff and reads as a clip; sole rim is a fat bevel that makes the toe look like a thimble.

## item-studio-3: REJECTED (closest of the batch, but not there)
- Strap construction is illogical: the left shoulder strap is a folded flap with a grey triangular hole at the base that matches no real cut; reads as a construction error at zoom and as a smudge at 96 px.
- Soft airbrushed gradients remain in the bodice (right shoulder, under the band) and the skirt planes, against the stepped hard-edged technique of item-studio-1 and item-gala-2.
- Contour weight varies from about 12 px at the shoulder to a thin 3 px double line at the hem (hem has a faint inner second line), versus the even 7-10 px neighbours.
- Worn-body cues: bust cup curvature at the right shoulder and a whole-dress lean to the right, so it reads as a dress on an invisible body, not a flat-lay (brief must-not 12).
- Good and to keep: one-shoulder white, wrap panel with curved edge, asymmetric hem, white reads white (not cream or grey), alpha, scale and margins correct.

## key-art: REJECTED
- The single accent, the sky ribbon, is wrong in this image: it has a glossy satin sheen, a hairline 2 px contour (the tee and trousers use about 8 px), and it emerges from behind the trouser leg so it reads as a tail or tag attached to the trousers rather than a separate prop. The brief asked for flat, same-hand, muted #2b6a9a with a light plane #7db4d9.
- At 120 px the ribbon is a blue speck and adds nothing; the accent does not read.
- Composition is bottom-heavy and the shoes are scaled separately from the garments (brief said ONE uniform scale), so the pair looks oversized next to the trousers; the shoes sit tight to the trouser hem with no deliberate relationship.
- Positive: ground and title band are calm, pieces are accepted pixels, tee focal point is large enough, glaze matches accepted riviera.

## feature-graphic: REJECTED
- The only changed element is the boot pair, and it is the rejected item-rainy-city-5 (cylinders, lightning-bolt gloss streaks, taupe toe caps, thin soft contour compared with the trousers and umbrella). It is the weakest object in the image and visible in the 1024x500 store crop.
- The brief's fallback (b) was to paste accepted item-studio-5 instead of an unaccepted piece; that fallback was not used.
- Everything else is unchanged and fine: no seam visible at the patch, composition balanced.

## Result
Accepted: backdrop-studio. Rejected: item-rainy-city-5, item-studio-3, key-art, feature-graphic. Round-2 brief written at retry/round-2/brief.md.
