## item-rainy-city-5 (Black Rubber Boots): REJECTED
- Does not read as rubber boots. The pair reads as short chunky slip-on clogs or slippers: a broad paddle-shaped toe box, a very short shaft, and a rolled ring at the opening that looks like a mug rim or a hardware ring rather than a boot top. No tall waterproof leg, so the "waterproof" gameplay meaning is lost (rubric 8).
- The sole rim is a thick pale taupe bevel all round the toe and up the sides, not the thin 10-14 px dark rim requested. It reads as a grey platform, the same trait as item-studio-5 which the brief said to differ from; it also makes the black body look like a cut-out sitting on a tray.
- Style (rubric 3): the contour is a smooth, near-uniform line with soft low-contrast brushed fill and a soft rubbed pale patch at the instep. It lacks the pressure-varied marker line and hard-edged tone planes of item-studio-5 and item-riviera-5. At 48 px it is two black blobs with tails (pull loops), indistinguishable from slippers.
- The pull-loop tab hangs below the cuff like a key tab.

## item-studio-3 (White Drape Dress): REJECTED
- Construction does not read as a flat-laid dress (rubric 2). The skirt is a cone leaning right with a ruler-straight right flank and a pointed top-left strap, and the whole garment slants like a wind-blown or worn dress, the exact fault the brief listed. The waist narrowing exists only on the left; the right is a straight diagonal.
- The "band" is a large cape-like flap over the right shoulder/hip with a stubby squared right corner and a beige triangle at its end; it does not read as a twisted band or a plausible wrap. The wrap overlap line plus flap looks like two unrelated seams.
- The fold shading is soft, grey-beige, smudged and chevron-shaped (airbrushed look, must-not 3), not crisp opaque planes like item-studio-1.
- Contour: thin and fairly uniform around the skirt and noticeably lighter than item-gala-2 at 96 and 128 px (contact sheet b); heavier dark contact wash on the right gives a sticker feel on dark backgrounds. At 48/96 px it collapses to a white triangle with a hook.
- Fill has some paper tooth but overall looks like a vector fashion flat next to the painted neighbours (rubric 3).

- Style drift inside the image (rubric 3, must-not 10): the pieces were resampled at three different factors (tee 0.65, trousers 0.51, shoes 0.36), so contour weights differ visibly: tee line about 6 px, trousers about 4 px, espadrilles about 3 px hairline. The shared uniform scale demanded by the brief is unmet (the manifest admits this). At 360 px the tee looks drawn with a marker, the trousers and shoes with a thinner pen.
- Composition: left-weighted, the espadrilles float as a small separate pair at the right of the trouser hem with a large empty right-middle and empty lower-right; the outfit is a catalogue product sheet rather than a hero. It fills about 57 percent of the height but only the tee has presence; at 120 px the shoes are two specks. The pieces have almost no contact shadow, so they sit pasted on the paper.
