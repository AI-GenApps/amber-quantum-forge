## item-rainy-city-5: REJECTED
- Still two cylinders: a dome toe joined to a straight tube. At 48 and 96 px it reads as two mittens or oven gloves, not wellingtons. No heel, no real ankle break, no open top, no sole edge (rubric 2, 4, 8).
- A hard-edged full-height vertical pale bar on every shaft reads as a glossy specular strip (rubric 7, must-not 2).
- Contour is a clean uniform vector line and fills are uniform with a faint hatch; does not match the pressure-varied hand of item-studio-5 (rubric 3, must-not 1).
- Cuff tabs are tiny rectangles that read as buckles.

## item-studio-3: REJECTED
- Contour is about 3 px hairline, uniform; accepted neighbours are 7 to 10 px and pressure varied. The dress looks drawn by a different artist, as a vector fashion flat (rubric 3).
- Fills are smooth and flat with no paper tooth or brush drag, unlike item-gala-2 and item-studio-1.
- Large straight-edged grey wedges run from band to hem and read as stripes or sails; at 48 px it collapses to a triangle with grey bars (rubric 4).
- Right flank is a ruler-straight diagonal, hem tip is a sharp point, waist narrowing is one-sided: reads wind-blown or cone-like, not flat-laid.
- Improvements acknowledged: no triangular strap hole, white reads white, alpha clean.

## key-art: REJECTED
- Look is too small and low-left: tee about 417 px (41%) wide, outfit about 30% of canvas area, about 60% empty paper. At 120 px wide it is a speck cluster (rubric 4, 6).
- Espadrilles read oversized next to the trousers and sit as a detached pair with a gap; the three pieces do not read as one outfit; composition is left-weighted (rubric 6, 8).
- The edge wash is a full-height straight vertical band on both sides: the listed straight-band glaze fault (rubric 7).
- Accepted pixels are consistent in hand and no ribbon is fine; title band empty; no text.

## feature-graphic: ACCEPTED

- Minor, not player-visible at display size: rainy look gets laced platform boots rather than wellingtons, acceptable as the Rainy City footwear in the poster.
