## key-art - REJECTED
- Does fix the round-1 faults: hat is now clear of the neckline (gap about 45 px), top 34 percent is empty, tee is the accepted raster with eight navy stripes, espadrilles are a matched pair, six items only, no text.
- Style drift remains (rubric 3 and 7). The contour on the hat, trousers, basket and ribbon is a heavy, uniform 12-15 px "sticker" underprint, roughly twice the weight of the accepted tee's thin contour (tee: about 4-5 px at this scale). It is also uneven: doubled/offset on the right leg of the trousers, thin on the tee. The set does not look like one hand.
- Interiors are still soft and photographic: trousers have airbrushed streak folds, the basket has a glossy tight-woven photographic weave and a shaded interior, the hat crown is a soft smudge, the ribbon has soft mottled shading. Not flat stepped gouache.
- Ribbon is bright cerulean (about #3a7fc0 range), louder than the muted accent #2b6a9a and visibly brighter than the navy tee.
- Edge glaze (left/right edges) is a grey-green cast with hard vertical band edges (corners measured about #c8c5b8), not a quiet sky glaze; reads as a dirty vertical stripe, and the linen is a flat smooth gradient with no visible weave. Sky colour does not read.
- Composition: the whole look is small (about 40 percent of canvas width) and sits left of centre with a large dead zone on the right and below the basket; at 120 px it is a tiny cluster with no strong focal point. Hat is not centred over the tee.

## backdrop-studio - REJECTED
- Fixed: violet glaze now reads clearly at 48 px (corners about #d1bece), calm core is pixel-identical to round 1, exactly four prop groups, left and right balanced, no text.
- Props are still photographic/3D (rubric 3, 7): the three pins have glossy spherical pearl heads with specular highlights and grey metallic shafts (the brief explicitly forbade spherical gloss); the chalk is a bevelled, rounded soap/eraser bar with an inner raised panel, not a chalk stick; the silk swatch is a shaded 3D folded cloth with pinked edges. Core flatness is fine, but the paper looks like digital texture, not gouache and linen weave.
