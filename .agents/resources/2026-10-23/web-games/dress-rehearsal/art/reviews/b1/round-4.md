## backdrop-studio: REJECTED
Technical: 1024x1536 RGB, no alpha, no border. The core (x 14-86, y 12-88) has mean (226,213,190) and range 219..233, which is inside spec. Technical checks pass.

Contact sheets: a-full.png, b-small.png and b-120.png (display size), c-side.png (beside accepted icon and tee), d-crop-props.png, d-swatch.png and d-weave.png (detail).

Why it fails:
1. Style break (rubric 3, 7). The accepted icon and tee are painterly gouache with marker contours and visible brush character. The props here read as flat vector clip-art. They are hard-edged polygons with no paper tooth or brush wobble, and the pins are straight lines with circles. Next to the accepted assets it looks like a different artist and a different tool.
2. Glaze fails the brief (revision 4). The lilac glaze is a near-straight vertical band with a soft gradient ramp on both sides. It runs the full height, with no meaningful thinning at mid-top or mid-bottom and no irregular hand-glazed edge. At 360 px wide it reads as two plain gradient stripes. There is a faint yellowish-lighter band at the inner edge (about x 90-130) between lilac and linen, which the brief explicitly forbade.
3. Weave and paper (revision 5). Horizontal streaking is still obvious in the full view and the 360 px view, so the grain reads as a horizontal scanline filter rather than an irregular warp/weft crossing. There is no convincing paper-tooth character inside the prop fills.
4. Violet swatch (revision 3). It has only 5 pinked teeth (brief: 6 to 8). The teeth are small and show stair-stepped pixel edges. The silhouette looks like a tent or bookmark, not a folded silk swatch. The flap and fold planes are geometric slivers.
5. Props are tiny and lost (revision 6). The pins, offcuts and chalk are far below the requested 10-15 percent of canvas width, so they read as stray specks at display size. The chalk is a plain beige rounded rectangle with a stripe, and at 360 px it reads as a blank card. Offcuts sit in the middle-upper left, the pins and swatch low, and the right side has only two props. Overall the layout is sparse and accidental.
6. As the style anchor for five other backdrops it would propagate the vector-prop look and the straight-band glaze. At display size it communicates only "beige page with purple edges", not a tailoring studio.

Accepted: none.
