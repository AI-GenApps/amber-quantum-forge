# b1 round 1 review

Contact sheets in this folder: a-full-on-grey, b-display-sizes, b2-icon-masks, c-consistency, d-tee-dark-linen-white, d2-tee-shadow-zoom, e-backdrop-props-zoom, e2-key-title-band, e3-key-hat-tee, f-icon-zoom. There were no previously accepted assets, so the tee, icon and key-art were compared against each other.

Technical: icon 1024x1024 RGB; key-art 1024x1536 RGB; backdrop-studio 1024x1536 RGB; item-riviera-1 1024x1024 RGBA, corners alpha 0, outer 4% border alpha 0, bbox 80% x 66%. All sizes and aspect classes correct, no alpha on opaque files.

## icon - ACCEPTED
Cream gouache hanger with brass hook and red bow on night ground. Silhouette is mirror-symmetric, hero inside the safe circle, reads at 48 px and under squircle and circle masks (b2). No text, no extra objects, no gloss blobs, ground is night with faint grain. Gouache brush character holds up at 2x zoom. Minor: little ink contour, but the brief asked for light ink only. Accepted.

## item-riviera-1 - ACCEPTED
Eight even torso stripes, sleeve stripes mirrored, white body (about #faf5eb) clearly white not grey, navy stripes (about #193368) read navy. Warm ink contour 7 to 10 px, flat stepped planes, one fold from left sleeve and one under the neckline. Real alpha, clean edge on dark, linen and white (d), shadow is a faint tight wash well under 18 percent and faded before the border. Reads at 64/96/128 px. Top-right corner empty. Bounding box 80% wide, 66% high, within the top maximum. Accepted as the style anchor.

## key-art - REJECTED
- Hat brim and crown overlap and hide the tee neckline (e3), violating "no piece covers another's key features".
- Style drift from the anchor: hat, trousers, basket, espadrilles and glasses have no ink contour and use fine photographic or airbrushed shading and micro-detail (individual straw strands, basket weave); the tee has the bold 7 to 10 px contour. Not one hand (c).
- The tee is not the accepted tee: nine stripes against eight, brighter royal blue, shorter boxy body.
- Sky ribbon is a loud saturated cobalt with satin highlights, louder than the navy tee and not the muted #2b6a9a accent.
- Corner and edge wash is blotchy and mottled, reaching into the title band; ground reads peach-tan rather than the linen colour.
- Good: title band is empty, composition and focal point idea, matched espadrille pair, scale of the look.

## backdrop-studio - REJECTED
- Violet #6a3fa0 wash is effectively absent: edge-to-core change is only about 4 to 10 levels towards grey-pink, no violet cast; the 48 px thumbnail is indistinguishable from plain linen, so the chapter colour does not read. This is the anchor for five other backdrops.
- Props are too faint and few: pin shafts are hairline and vanish at crop size; the silk reads as a grey-mauve rag; left side carries two groups, right side one (the chalk was removed).
- Good: the calm core is flat, even and within #d9ccb3 to #ebe0cc (measured min #dacdb5, max #e9dcc6), linen weave and grain are fine, no text, no vignette ring, props stay in the ring.

## Outcome
Accepted: icon, item-riviera-1. Rejected: key-art, backdrop-studio (round-2 brief written).
