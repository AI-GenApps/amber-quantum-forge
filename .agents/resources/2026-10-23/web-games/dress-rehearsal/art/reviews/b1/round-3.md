## key-art - REJECTED
Fixed since round 2 (keep): empty top 34 percent; hat-to-tee gap (~55 px) with full neckline; accepted tee reproduced; matched espadrille pair; trousers as two legs; six items, no text; ribbon now reads muted sky (`#2b6a9a` body, lighter twist plane).
Still wrong:
1. Not a "look", a scatter. Pieces float with 40-110 px dead gaps (hat, tee, trousers, basket, shoes, glasses, ribbon each isolated); the look's apparent width comes from spread, not from piece size. Tee is only ~355 px wide (35 percent of canvas); at 120 px wide the whole thing is a few tiny icons.
2. Style drift between pieces. Contour on hat, trousers, shoes, basket is a thin ~3 px near-uniform line; the tee's is 7-10 px with pressure variation, so tee looks pasted from another asset. Tee also has no contact wash while the other pieces have a soft grey drop-shadow, a different lighting treatment.
3. Hat is drawn in a tilted three-quarter perspective with a smooth airbrushed light/shade transition and a flat diagonal terminator across the crown; not the flat-lay overhead camera and not stepped gouache. Hat has a single crown contour that breaks open on the left.
4. Sunglasses: tiny glossy black blob with a bevelled grey rim and light-grey translucent lens; reads as a sticker, wobbly lumpy edge, not cat-eye at display size.
5. Espadrilles: soft glossy white highlight patches (plastic sheen), shoes lean toward the viewer more than other pieces. Trousers: fold planes are sharp spiky shards (tan wedges) rather than two deliberate soft fold planes; reads as a camouflage pattern.
6. Basket: double handle with misaligned back handle, 3/4 perspective lean, brown weave squares of differing tone read toy-like; it is the same lean the rule allows but the weave is uniform and mushy at 120 px.
7. Ground: edge glaze is grey/blue-grey at the left/right edges (mid-row about `#cbcfce`) with a visible yellowish band between glaze and linen core (core x 120-150 is yellower than the core), so it does not read as a sky wash; the weave is a regular horizontal-stripe digital texture.
8. Reads as generic AI clip-art flat-lay, not the award-calibre editorial gouache spread.

## backdrop-studio - REJECTED
Fixed since round 2 (keep): props are now flat with no ink contour; pins are flat with no highlight dot (good); calm core is clean (luminance range 203-217, mean `#e2d5be`); lilac glaze hue is right (corners about `#d9c6d4`); four groups, no text, props only left/right.
Still wrong:
1. Pattern-paper offcuts touch/cross the left canvas edge (x=0) and are cropped; they must sit fully inside the canvas.
2. Chalk stick still has a darker side face (reads as 3D bar with a lower side plane) and a thin pale rim line; brief asked for one flat plane plus a flat darker stripe along the length and no side face, and it is small (~100 px).
3. Violet swatch: zigzag edge is a stair-stepped pixel sawtooth (visible jaggies), not a hand-pinked edge; the fold plane is a muddy grey-brown (`#8a7a96`-ish, looks like a cast shadow) rather than a deep violet plane; the two violet planes are nearly the same flat tone.
4. Glaze: it is a straight regular vertical band down both sides (not irregular, no corner emphasis, no drift in and out), with a visible lighter yellow band between lilac and linen (halo) instead of fading to plain linen. At 48 px it reads as two stripes.
5. Paper texture: regular horizontal scanline weave, looks like a digital filter, not gouache paper with grain; no paper tooth in the painted fills.
6. Props are very small and all hug the edges; the set feels sparse and cut-and-paste, not painted by the hand that made the icon and tee. As style anchor for 5 more backdrops this is not strong enough.
