# Dress Rehearsal b5 round 1 review

Technical (all 11 files): 1024x1024 RGBA, all four corners alpha 0, outer 4 percent border alpha 0, top-right 20 percent square empty, centred, no baked checkerboard, no visible halo on linen or night. Bounding boxes within category maxima and touching a limit on at least one axis. Contact sheets: a-all-grey, b-display-linen (128/96/64), c-accepted-set, c-pair1/2/3, d-dark-light, zoom crops z-*.

Verdicts: 9 accepted, 2 rejected.

## item-studio-6 Magenta Geometric Bag - ACCEPT
Reads magenta (not pink, not violet), three flat stepped planes, crisp facets, handle attached at two clear points, no text/clasp. Same facet language as accepted item-studio-1. Strong silhouette at 64 px.

## item-studio-7 Blush Sculpted Felt Hat - ACCEPT
Pale warm pink, clean single brim fold on the left, flat right, crown pinch clear, fuzzy felt edge. Same lean and contour as accepted hats. Shading is slightly softer than the stepped siblings but within tolerance; reads at 64 px. Distinct from vintage-7 by colour and folded brim.

## item-studio-8 Chrome Sculpture Cuff - ACCEPT
Open C-shaped cuff with one plausible half-twist, metallic grey with top-left highlight band, no gems/glints/clasp. Smooth chrome blends are the only soft-gradient area in the batch but justified by the material and subtle at display size. Reads as metal accessory at 48/64 px.

## item-vintage-1 Cream Pussy-Bow Blouse - REJECT
Near-duplicate of accepted item-gala-3 (same collar and bow knot, same yoke gathers, same placket, same sleeve and cuff shape, same cream). Tails are a little longer and the sleeves a little fuller, but at 96 px (c-pair3-96) the two are indistinguishable, failing rubric point 8 and the brief's explicit "clearly distinct from the Gala silk blouse" requirement. Construction otherwise clean. Needs a redesign of bow scale, sleeve volume and shoulder/collar treatment (see round-2 brief).

## item-vintage-2 Rust Flared Corduroys - REJECT
Construction flaw: a stray rectangular tab floats above the centre of the waistband and is not attached to a logical loop; only two belt loops sit on the waistband. Wales are uniform, high-contrast stripes, so it reads as awning or pinstripe trousers rather than corduroy (no ridge softness). Also adds side pockets and crease lines that the brief did not ask for and that compete with the wales. Colour (rust-orange), flare, leg symmetry and scale are fine.

## item-vintage-3 Mustard Maxi Dress - ACCEPT
Mustard yellow (not orange/lemon), symmetrical deep V, matched flutter sleeves, fitted bodice with waist seam, A-line skirt with soft folds, evenly spaced daisy-and-dot hem band in rust and cream following the hem curve. No text. Rendering matches accepted dresses. Edge daisies sit tight to the hem silhouette but are invisible at display size.

## item-vintage-4 Tan Suede Fringe Jacket - ACCEPT
Tan suede with matte dab texture, pointed collar, 4 snap buttons, 8 yoke strips and about 10 stepped strips per sleeve, tidy and evenly spaced; hip length, no belt, distinct from the camel trench. Fringe is flat shingled strips rather than strands but is consistent with the flat gouache language and reads instantly at 96 px.

## item-vintage-5 Red Platform Clogs - ACCEPT
Exactly two mirrored clogs, parallel, non-overlapping, toes up; red leather upper with 5 brass studs each, wooden platform and block heel visible. Clear silhouette and colour at 64 px, distinct from black platform boots (mountain). Brush texture on the leather is lively but within the gouache look.

## item-vintage-6 Olive Patchwork Shoulder Bag - ACCEPT
Eight rectangular patches in three olive tones with tan stitch dashes, brass stud, strap loops with D-rings at both top corners, symmetrical. Reads yellow-green olive, not forest or khaki. Same rendering as accepted bags.

## item-vintage-7 Navy Floppy Felt Hat - ACCEPT
Reads navy blue (not black), centre-dent crown, lighter navy band, wavy drooping brim that dips on one side, same lean as accepted hats. Distinct from the cream sun hat and the blush felt hat.

## item-vintage-8 Teal Bead Necklace - ACCEPT
20 distinct round beads, 3 larger deep-teal accents, visible thread knots, teardrop loop with a top overlap that stays readable, no merging, no pendant or clasp. Reads teal at 48 px. Bead highlights are a little glossier than "glossless" but stay as flat painted dabs.
