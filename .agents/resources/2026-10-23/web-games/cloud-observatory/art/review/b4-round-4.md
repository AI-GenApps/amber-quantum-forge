# Cloud Observatory b4 round 4 (final) - review

## instrument-aurora-coil: REJECTED

Technical: PASS. 1024x1024 RGBA, corners and 32px border alpha 0, bbox x259-764 y114-901 (77% of height, baseline 901), 212k opaque px (within set range 80k-374k), no halo or checkerboard, clean on ink-900, parchment and magenta (contact sheet d). Valid file.

Fixed since round 3: the ribbon is now a real flat band with two edges, a twist and a teal/green/rose scheme; it reads as a ribbon, not smoke; body mass is comparable to siblings; ribbon sits in an open brass cup.

Why it still fails:
1. Palette and over-saturation (rubric 3, 7). Copper highlights sample at #F97A34 / #FF7F1F (hot orange) against the specified #B8693F / #7E4126. The glass cylinder is a saturated cyan-blue with white streak highlights, which reads glossy and plastic. In the set sheet (c) the coil is by far the loudest object and the only one whose colours are not the muted watercolour/satin-brass family of the orrery, beacon and telescope.
2. Construction (rubric 2). The copper winding is a stack of 15 identical closed hoops, with no pitch, so it reads as ribbed rings on a can rather than one continuous helical wire. The glass shows a flat vertical brass rod and the hoops sit in front of it with no convincing wrap. Stiff, mechanical, uniform.
3. Ribbon origin and rendering (brief, rubric 7). It enters the cup as a wide, flat, cut-off band instead of starting thin and widening. The yellow-green-rose gradient is a candy/iridescent blend, not distinct layered watercolour glazes, and the lime-yellow band is off-palette. It looks like a sticker laid on the cap. At 48px the ribbon is only about 18px wide, a small colour squiggle on top of a striped cylinder.
4. Consistency (rubric 3). The brass base and cap match the set. The coil body (glass plus copper) is smoother and more digital than the accepted pieces, and its hot colour stripes dominate the silhouette.

Verdict: rejected. This is the final round, so no further brief. The slot stays a placeholder.
