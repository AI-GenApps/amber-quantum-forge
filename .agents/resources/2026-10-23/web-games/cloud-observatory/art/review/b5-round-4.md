## lore-1 - REJECTED
- Sky/nebula: the 2x crop still shows serrated, polygon-lobed cells and a coarse crumpled texture. Accepted key-art has smooth glazes. This is the exact failure named in the round 3 and round 4 briefs.
- The nebula is mirrored left to right into a symmetric butterfly shape that points at the horizon. Real glazes are not symmetric, and it reads as a stamped or reflected composite.
- Purpose and brief miss: this is a full night scene. There is no wash-rose and gold dusk band at the horizon, so it does not read as "The Roof at Dusk".
- Key under the slate: only a tiny brass ring is visible under a floating, flat, parallelogram slate. The slate looks pasted on, with no believable gap or thickness. It does not read as a key under a lifted slate at display size.
- The dust sheet over the telescope is good, and the matte brass, muted teal lens and tripod contact shadows are improved. The caption band is calm and dark. These do not outweigh the sky failure.
- Verdict: reject. Final round, so the slot gets a placeholder.
