# Cloud Observatory b1 round 3 review

Technical: icon 1024x1024 RGB opaque OK. emblem-lyre 1024x1024 RGBA OK, corners/border alpha 0, nothing opaque outside r482, disc fully opaque. Contact sheets: a_full, b_display, c_consistency, d_alpha, zooms e/f in this folder.

## icon: REJECTED
Progress: dome is now seated on stone, bigger (about 42 percent width), star about 17 percent, swirl-oil texture is gone from the gold, matte foil with crackle matches the accepted dome. Still fails:
- Construction: the "building" is a bare rectangular stone pillar/chimney with a meaningless grey wedge fragment sticking out at the left of the parapet (a floating slab, not the accepted slate roof edge). It reads as a dome on a plinth/chimney, not the accepted keeper's house + terrace.
- The telescope tube is pasted on the dome face, left of the slit, instead of rising out of the open slit like the accepted building; it is short, stubby and has an odd collar block, so it does not read as a telescope and does not visibly aim into the star.
- Dome and brass have no dip-pen ink contour and no ribbed shading/shade side matching the accepted dome (accepted dome has ink contour, shaded right, parchment rim); icon dome looks like flat cut foil, not one artist with the building.
- Star is still a clean faceted vector eight-point star with hard straight edges (clip-art), only two flat halves; not cut foil.
- Sky: the teal/rose blooms have blocky, polygonal, pixel-stepped edges (visible at 1x on the left bloom); the brief required smooth watercolour.
- Stone masonry is a different, flatter rendering than the accepted building's stone (no stone-shade side, no rim light).
At 48 px it reads (gold dome + star on navy) but the flaws above are visible at 96 px and in the circle mask.

## emblem-lyre: REJECTED
Progress: frame radii correct and clean circle, smooth navy disc with fine grain and faint teal/rose blooms, five strings, symmetrical arms, scroll holes identical, engraved arcs added, gold now has crackle and mild tone variation, no gloss streak.
Still fails (this is the anchor of 12 emblems, highest bar):
- Stray artefact: an opaque pinkish-white blob on the left arm at about (372, 383) (the "parchment dab" became a hard dirty dot; no soft matte accent). Visible on every background.
- Jagged, stair-stepped, aliased silhouette edges on the arms, scrolls, crossbar and strings (pixel stepping visible at 3x zoom, ragged string edges): cut-out was not clean; the bible requires a crisp painted edge.
- Sound box still reads as a boat hull/gondola: a crescent with upswept pointed ends meeting the arms at sharp corners, with parallel keel lines. The brief asked for a deep rounded U-shaped resonator bowl with arms rising as continuous curves.
- Two-step foil is barely there: gold-light vs gold-shade planes (top-left vs bottom-right) are almost absent on arms, scrolls and outer ring; ring has no clear gold-light top-left line or shade line bottom-right. Largely flat tone with crackle.
- Strings are uneven in width/brightness (left string pale and ragged).
Alpha, frame radii and disc are fine and should be kept.
