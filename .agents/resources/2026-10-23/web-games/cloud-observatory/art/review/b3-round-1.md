# Review b3 round 1 (emblems: fox, key, harp, tower, owl)

Technical (all five): 1024x1024 RGBA, corners alpha 0, alpha 0 everywhere outside r 481, alpha 255 inside r 478. Frame (r 436-484 zone) is pixel-identical to accepted emblem-lyre (mean abs diff 0.0), disc rim colour identical. No text, glyphs, stars or lines. Clean on dark and light backgrounds (sheet d). Contact sheets: a (full grey), b (48/96/144 on ink-800), c (with 7 accepted emblems), d (dark/light), z-* zooms.

## emblem-fox - ACCEPTED
Left-facing seated profile, two ears, narrow muzzle with nose dot, small calm eye, tail connected to the haunch and curling up with a light tip, gold-light chest. Anatomy clean, no extra legs. Fur strokes are few and engraved; scale-crackle matches whale/serpent. Figure 475x513. Reads as a fox with a huge brush at 48 px.

## emblem-key - ACCEPTED
Round bow with inner ring and trefoil of three circles, straight shaft, two collar bands, exactly one rectangular tooth at lower right. No chain/tag. Same emboss and gold leaf as lantern/crown. Figure 270x533. Reads instantly at 48 px.

## emblem-harp - ACCEPTED
Right pillar with capital and foot, curved neck with one small curl, diagonal sound-box, exactly seven graded strings (confirmed by count at zoom), no crossing, no hands/wings. Strings terminate on the sound-box edge. Slightly asymmetric silhouette is inherent to a lap harp. Figure 392x535. Reads as a harp at 48 px and is distinct from the lyre (U shape).

## emblem-tower - ACCEPTED
Two stepped tiers, arched door on the threshold, one slit window per tier (door and lower window clearly separated), conical roof, thin spire with small pennant streaming right (no text). Body symmetrical, masonry is a few block lines. Figure 327x533. Reads as a tower at 48 px.

## emblem-owl - ACCEPTED
Frontal, symmetrical, two ear tufts, two matched concentric-ring eyes with no highlights, small beak, three scallops per wing, three toes per foot gripping a branch stub, fan tail below. Slightly chunky/toy-like compared with whale/serpent but within the accepted emboss family and no worse than lantern; reads clearly at 48 px. Minor note only, not a blocker.

## Set consistency
Palette, emboss depth, gold crackle, paper grain, top-left light and frame match accepted b1/b2 emblems. Silhouettes are distinct from each other and from the other seven at 48 px.
