# Review b2 round 1

Technical: all PNGs correct size; emblems RGBA with alpha 0 corners and zero alpha outside r482; emblem frames pixel-identical to accepted emblem-lyre (ring and hairline region max diff 0); sky-far opaque RGB, no alpha; nebula-mid RGBA, outer border alpha 0, coverage 12.7 percent, peak about 24 percent; no pure black/white except 2 near-black pixels in hills. Contact sheets in this folder (a_*, b_*, d_*, e_*, z_*).

## Verdicts

- sky-far: ACCEPTED. Calm navy ramp ink-950 to ink-700, faint horizon haze, 84 dim pigment dots, nothing figure-like, grain matches key-art. Header and tab bands calm. (Teal haze is very faint but allowed.)
- nebula-mid: ACCEPTED. Soft teal and rose wet-in-wet washes on a loose diagonal, same character as the accepted key-art nebula, low opacity (max about 24 percent), centre band quiet, bottom 30 percent clear, no stars or hard edges. Stacked with sky-far and the accepted building on a 360x780 mock it reads calm and does not hurt star contrast.
- horizon-hills: REJECTED. (1) Ridges stop 32 px from the left and right edges and 47 px from the bottom: hard vertical slab edges that show bare sky on any screen wider than 2:3, not a full-width bottom-anchored layer. (2) Crest rim is a crisp 2 px near-opaque bright teal line (about #5F8FAE, alpha 95 percent), reading as a vector outline/halo instead of a 40 percent watercolour rim. (3) Near-ridge shelf is a plain dome, ridge edges are smooth vector-like curves. Value steps, pines and grain are good.
- emblem-whale: ACCEPTED. Clear profile, spout, forked flukes, matches lyre gold leaf and frame. Minor: about 8 throat grooves instead of 5 to 6 and a slightly friendly face; invisible at 48 to 144 px and within style.
- emblem-archer: ACCEPTED. Bow, taut string and diagonal arrow with three vanes read instantly at 48 px; construction is consistent (arrow perpendicular to the bow axis, string meets tips and nock). Slim string is lighter than siblings but the silhouette carries.
- emblem-crown: ACCEPTED. Symmetrical, three balled front points plus two rear tips, rope band, satin gold matching lyre. Reads at 48 px.
- emblem-serpent: ACCEPTED. Clean S-curve, even body, lozenge scales, belly stripe, forked tongue, tail clear of the hairline ring by 76 px. Strong silhouette; fits the set.
- emblem-lantern: ACCEPTED. Symmetrical cage, ring, domed cap, flame on oil pan, tinted panes. Minor: faint pale mottling on the two side panes reads slightly as smudges; not noticeable at display size. Reads as a lantern at 48 px.
- emblem-ship: REJECTED. Hull silhouette is a lumpy bean with a dent in the keel line (looks like a clam, not a seaworthy hull); bow/stern logic is ambiguous (stay line to the raised left tip while a spar and the sail tack extend right, so the sail billows against the apparent heading, and the right spar grows out of the sail corner rather than the hull). Frame, gold and waves are fine.
