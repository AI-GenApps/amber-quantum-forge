Result: 0 of 4 accepted. All four rejected. No accepted assets exist yet, so there was no accepted set to compare against; consistency was judged against the art bible and between the four deliveries.

Technical checks (PIL): all files present, correct size and mode.

Technical is fine everywhere; the rejections are about art quality and style.

## Shared problems (apply to all four)
1. A "painterly filter" texture: the whole image is covered in a tessellated, swirl-polygon pattern (visible on sky, ground, stone, gold, emblem disc at zoom). It reads as an AI oil-paint/Kuwahara filter, not cold-press paper grain plus transparent watercolour. The bible wants fine ~3 px paper grain and smooth wet-in-wet washes with blooms.
2. Gold is not the bible ramp: emblem gold averages about #ECBA46 (saturated lemon-yellow, brighter than gold-leaf #D8A94B); lyre and ring show bevel/emboss shading with soft specular highlights (plastic/vinyl gloss) rather than matte foil with 2 steps and crackle.
3. Line treatment: the building has only a hairline dark contour, not the 3-5 px broken pen contour thicker on the shadow side; the lyre has no engraved gold-deep detail on the arms.

## icon - REJECTED
- Purpose/composition: telescope points up-left but the star is up-right, so the telescope aims away from the star; the one idea (observatory looks at a star) is lost.
- Construction: the slate roof is drawn as a steep pyramid seen from above and fills the whole lower left and right, wrong perspective versus the eye-level building, and the corners are not quiet wash as briefed. The dome sits on a stub of wall with no logic.
- Star: flat clip-art eight-ray star with a flat pale disc core (reads as a sun badge), not hand-laid gold leaf; the teal/rose bloom behind it has a hard-ish mottled edge.
- Style: the sky is the best watercolour of the batch, but the dome/roof is crisp cartoon, the star is vector; three techniques in one image.
- Readable at 48 px: yes (dome + star), which is why it is close, but it is not award calibre.

## key-art - REJECTED
- Texture: nebula and sky show heavy mosaic/oil-filter noise (zoom confirms); ridge and ground are scribbly. Not wet-in-wet watercolour.
- Value/focus: the stone walls are the brightest, most saturated mass (lavender) and outshine the gold dome and lamp; the gold star above the dome is a 6 px dot that disappears at 360 px, so the telescope has no visible target.
- Building: lavender marbled stone with pink and teal blotches reads as opal, not cool stone; scale OK (about 29 percent of height, base at 72 percent).
- Stars: a trio of dots near (520,148) in the title band reads as a mini constellation.
- Safe areas OK: title band dark, button band dark and calm. Pines: three, fine.

## observatory-building - REJECTED
- Stone: walls are mottled lavender, rose and teal blotches (looks like a marble/opal game tile), saturated and busy; not cool stone/stone-shade with a teal fill only in shadows.
- Line: hairline contour instead of the specified 3-5 px pen contour; the sprite reads as a glossy cartoon/mobile-casual asset rather than an ink-and-watercolour atlas plate.
- Construction: base line slopes (right front corner sits lower and flares outward), front gable and right tower meet without a convincing roof junction; the right-hand side wall is nearly as wide as the front so the 10 degree view is closer to 30-35 degrees.
- Dome and telescope are the strongest part (clean cap, ribs, slit aligned) but the hammered-foil texture is tessellated.
- Positives to keep: silhouette, proportions, chimney top-left, lit window, door, dome construction, transparent edge, scale.

## emblem-lyre - REJECTED (anchor of 12 emblems, so held to the highest bar)
- Gold: lemon-yellow, beveled/embossed with specular highlights (3D vinyl look); the bible asks for matte leaf with gold-light/gold-leaf/gold-shade steps, faint crackle, engraved lines.
- Symmetry: the two arm/scroll junctions are not mirror images (left has a seam/crack and a different knob at the crossbar join; right differs); scroll holes differ in size. Bible: lyre arms must be symmetrical.
- Detail: only two engraved lines on the sound box; arms and scrolls carry no engraving; no parchment accent highlight as briefed.
- Disc: the navy wash carries the tessellated filter pattern; teal and rose blooms are fine and low opacity.
- Positives to keep: frame radii (ring 450-480, hairline 428-434), transparent corners, five strings, composition, readability at 48/96/144 px.
