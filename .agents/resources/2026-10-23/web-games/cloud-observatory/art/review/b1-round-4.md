# Cloud Observatory b1 round 4 (final) review

Technical: icon 1024x1024 RGB opaque OK. emblem-lyre 1024x1024 RGBA, all four corners alpha 0, opaque bbox (32,32)-(992,992), disc fully opaque, clean edges on dark, parchment and magenta (sheet d). Sheets: a_full_*, b_display, c_consistency, d_alpha, zooms e/f/g.

## icon: REJECTED
Progress: the diagonal slate slice is gone, masonry now has block lines, lit coping and a dark right face closer to the accepted terrace; dome, foil crackle, ink contour and scale match the accepted building; corners are quiet; it reads at 48 px (gold dome + star on navy).
Still fails:
- The teal/rose bloom still has blocky, polygonal, pixel-stepped edges (clearly visible at 1x and in zoom e). The brief named this a hard shared fix; it is not fixed. Rubric 7 and 5.
- The star is still a clean faceted vector eight-point star with hard straight edges and only a hint of foil texture; it reads as clip-art next to the painted dome, and the telescope tube overlaps its core, so the hero star is partly hidden (focal point muddied).
- The telescope tube lies across the dome face to the left of the slit instead of rising out of the open slit like the accepted building; it still looks pasted on, with a blocky collar.
- The base is still a bare free-standing stone pillar with one window, not the keeper's-house terrace; it reads as a tower or lighthouse, not the accepted building.
Final round: stays rejected, placeholder icon needed.

## emblem-lyre: ACCEPTED
Fixed since round 3: the pink blob is gone; silhouette edges on arms, scrolls, crossbar and strings are now clean and anti-aliased (no stair-stepping at 3x); the sound box is now a deep rounded bowl with arms rising as continuous curves; strings are even; foil has crackle and modest light/shade; frame radii correct, disc smooth navy with faint teal/rose blooms, fine paper grain; gold matches the accepted dome foil; no text, no contour (correct for emblems).
Residual flaws (minor, not visible at 144 px or below): faint mirrored centre seam in the bowl texture, bowl highlights are symmetric rather than top-left keyed, and the bowl reads slightly lid-like at the top. Reads instantly as a lyre at 48, 96 and 144 px and is usable as the anchor for the other 11 emblems (future emblems should add top-left light/bottom-right shade more clearly).
