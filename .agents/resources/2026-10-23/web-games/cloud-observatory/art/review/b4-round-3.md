# Review b4 round 3 (instruments)

Technical: all 8 files present, 1024x1024, RGBA, four corners alpha 0, outer 3 percent border alpha 0, base line y=901 for all, long side 75 to 81 percent of canvas (telescope 807, lens 832 wide, prism 811, orrery 808, star-map 774, beacon 814, archive 775, coil 815). Contact sheets in this folder (a_full_grey_1, b_display, b_display_x2, c_consistency, d_dark, d_light, z_* detail crops).

Set note: the seven accepted instruments share ink contour, satin brass ramp, top-left key light and grain, and sit well beside the accepted observatory-building. Silhouettes are distinct at 48 px.

- instrument-telescope: ACCEPTED. Single bold diagonal on three umber legs, small finder with two brackets, collars, one small dust-sheet corner on one leg. Clean construction, brass matches building.
- instrument-lens: ACCEPTED. Three true circles graded large-medium-small on a bar with two feet, each with a tightening screw and parchment highlight arc. Glass is a slightly flat grey-teal but fine. Low height by design.
- instrument-prism: ACCEPTED (marginal). Reads instantly as triangle plus colour fan at 48 px, beam and fan geometry consistent, four muted bands. Minor: a few dry-brush white chips on the glass and no visible depth to the prism; not noticeable at 112 px.
- instrument-orrery: ACCEPTED. Sun ball with crackle, three tilted rings, three planets each on an arm, crank, gear teeth on base. Reads as a target on a base.
- instrument-star-map: ACCEPTED. Heavy block with screw and T-bar, blank vellum with three gold dots, roller at side. Navy-iron plus brass stays in palette; no text or lines.
- instrument-beacon: ACCEPTED. Tall post on three feet, cone lantern with lamp-glow core, beam wedge ends well inside the canvas, blank hanging shutter plate. Thin post but the T plus wedge silhouette reads.
- instrument-archive: ACCEPTED. 3x5 aligned drawers, blank label holders, scroll with no writing, brass rail at side. Heaviest asset but within the size rules.
- instrument-aurora-coil: REJECTED. The "aurora ribbon" is a faint grey-teal wisp from a pinhole on the cap and reads as smoke or steam (brief says no smoke); low contrast on navy, almost no green or rose, tiny compared with siblings; coil assembly is small and squat so the whole object reads as a pot with steam at 48 and 112 px and looks lighter than the set. Brass base, glass cylinder and copper turns are good and should be kept. See b4/round-4/brief.md.
