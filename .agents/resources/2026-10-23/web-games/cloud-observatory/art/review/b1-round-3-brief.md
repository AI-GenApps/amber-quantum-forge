## icon


- Purpose: the app icon. Must say "observatory + a bright star in a deep blue night" instantly at 48 px. Shown through adaptive masks (circle, squircle, rounded square) next to polished store icons.
- Subject: one hero composition. The accepted observatory's brass dome with its open slit and telescope tip, seen close and cropped (dome cap and the top of the slate roof only, no full house), at the lower centre; the telescope points up-left toward ONE large luminous gold star (a soft eight-ray hand-painted gold-leaf star, rays of two lengths, with gold-light core) at the upper right of the dome, about 16 percent of the canvas width. Behind: a deep navy wash (ink-950 at the top to ink-700 at the base) with a single soft teal-and-rose watercolour bloom around the star and 3 to 5 tiny dim star dots. Three major shapes only: dome, star, wash.
- Composition: full-bleed square, painted to every edge. ALL essential content (dome, telescope tip, star) inside the central circle of 62 percent of the canvas width (about 635 px diameter), centred; the focal gold star at about (62%, 36%), the dome at about (44%, 68%). Corner areas carry quiet wash and paper grain only (masks will crop them).
- Values: the star and the gold dome are the brightest mass, the sky dark, so the pair reads at 48 px. Keep a 40 percent value gap between the dome's lit side and the sky.
- Must-nots: no text or letters, no pre-rounded corners, no border, no inner frame, no glossy app-icon gradient or bevel, no glow halo bigger than the star, no lens flare, no sparkle clusters, no constellation lines, no moon, no people.

### Round 3 revision notes for icon (round 2 verdict: REJECTED)

What is wrong:
- The dome floats in empty sky on a thin stepped slab with a tiny roof wedge; there is no building under it, so the construction is illogical and it reads as a bell or cloche on a shelf, not an observatory.
- The whole motif is small and the icon is mostly empty sky: dome about 28 percent of the canvas width, star 15 percent; at 48 px the dome is a tiny blob. Weak, not award calibre.
- The star is a faceted clip-art eight-point star, not cut gold foil; the gold of star and dome and the sky carry a visible swirl-polygon oil-filter texture.
- Roof wedge at the dome base is a meaningless fragment.

What to change:
- Ground the dome. Show the accepted building's dome seated on its stone terrace parapet (copy the accepted dome, collar, ribs, slit and telescope exactly), with the parapet/top of the stone wall running off the bottom of the central circle (cropped, not a floating slab), and at most a small slate roof edge at the left, attached to the parapet like in the accepted building. No floating pieces.
- Scale up: dome about 40 to 46 percent of the canvas width, centred at about (46%, 64%); the star about 17 to 19 percent of the canvas width. Everything essential stays inside the central 62 percent circle (635 px).
- The tube must keep visibly aiming at the star centre (round 2 aim was correct: keep the star on the telescope axis up-left of the dome, about (36%, 38%)). Keep three major shapes: dome-on-parapet, star, wash.
- Star: soft hand-cut gold foil, eight rays of two lengths with slightly uneven cut edges, two tonal steps only (gold-light core fading to gold-leaf, gold-shade on the lower-right facets), NOT a faceted/embossed vector star, no pale disc.
- Keep the round-2 sky (smooth navy watercolour, small teal and rose bloom behind the star, 3 to 5 tiny dim dots) but with no swirl texture; corners quiet wash.
- Same dip-pen ink contour and matte foil as the accepted building so the icon and the building look like one artist.

## emblem-lyre


- Purpose: the anchor of the 12 constellation emblems (Atlas card, 144 x 144 CSS px, also 48 and 96 px in lists). Defines the frame of art bible 8.2 for all the others.
- Subject: the Lyre. A classical U-shaped lyre in frontal elevation: two curving arms rising from a rounded sound-box base, joined by a crossbar near the top, FIVE straight strings running from the crossbar to the base, small scroll-curl ends at the top of each arm. Gold-leaf with engraved lines (gold-deep), a single parchment accent highlight on the top-left arm. Perfectly symmetrical left-right in construction; only the painted light differs.
- Composition: the frame exactly as art bible 8.2: transparent 1024x1024; outer ring r 450 to 480 px; navy watercolour disc; hairline ring r 428 to 434; the lyre centred with a bounding box about 500 px tall by 400 px wide; transparent outside r 480 px (corners and the 32 px border fully alpha 0).
- Must-nots: no text, no zodiac or musical-note glyphs, no stars, no constellation dots or lines, no extra ornaments, no ring tick marks, no inner glow, no drop shadow, no mirror gloss, no more than 5 strings, no asymmetry between the two arms, no hands or figures.

### Round 3 revision notes for emblem-lyre (round 2 verdict: REJECTED; this is the anchor of 12 emblems, held to the highest bar)

What is wrong:
- Gold is a single flat tone: no gold-light on top-left planes and no gold-shade on bottom-right planes for either the lyre or the outer ring (bible 8.2 asks for a gold-light line top-left and an inner shadow line bottom-right on the ring). It reads as a flat vector cut-out stuck on a painted disc.
- A glossy white specular strip on the upper-left arm (plastic gloss). The brief asked for a small matte parchment accent, not a shiny streak.
- The sound box reads as a boat hull or a basket: a flat flanged top rim with a flared boat-shaped bowl and parallel hull lines. It must read instantly as a lyre at 48 px.
- Fine swirl-polygon texture is still visible inside the gold and across the navy disc at zoom.
- The outer ring edge is slightly wobbly; keep it a clean circle (r 450 to 480).

What to change:
- Paint the foil with two clear tonal steps: gold-light (#EBCB85) planes on the top-left of the arms, scrolls and ring, gold-shade (#A47C30) on the bottom-right, base #D8A94B; faint crackle only; no bevel, no emboss, no gloss.
- Replace the specular streak with one small matte parchment (#F3E9D2) dab on the top-left arm, no hard white edge.
- Redraw the sound box as a rounded resonator: a deep U-shaped bowl (round bottom, no flat flange or gunwale rim) from which the two arms rise as continuous curves; three engraved arcs that follow the bowl curve, 3 to 4 px in gold-deep; the strings end at the crossbar and at the top of the bowl. Keep exactly five straight strings, perfect left-right mirror construction, same scrolls with identical holes, scroll-curl ends.
- Remove the texture: smooth watercolour disc (soft teal bloom upper left, rose bloom lower right, each under 25 percent) with fine paper grain; keep the frame radii exactly (ring 450-480, hairline 428-434 in gold-shade, transparent outside r480 including the 32 px border).
- Match the foil of the accepted observatory-building dome (same ramp, same matte finish).

### Round 2 revision notes for emblem-lyre

- Gold: use the bible ramp, matte hand-laid foil: base #D8A94B, lit planes #EBCB85 only on the top-left, shade #A47C30 on bottom-right, faint crackle and flake edges; NO bevel/emboss, no specular highlights, no lemon-yellow; the outer ring must use the same foil.
- Symmetry: construct the lyre as an exact mirror (same scroll, same hole size, same junction knob, no seam or crack on the left arm); only the painted light may differ left to right.

- Disc: remove the tessellated texture from the navy wash; smooth watercolour with fine paper grain, soft teal bloom upper left and rose bloom lower right under 25 percent opacity.
- Keep exactly: frame radii (ring 450-480 with inner shadow line bottom-right and gold-light line top-left, hairline 428-434 in gold-shade), five strings, figure bbox about 500 x 400 px centred, transparent outside r480.
