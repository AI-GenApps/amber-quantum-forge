# Review: batch b5, round 1

Technical: all 7 files present, correct sizes (lore 1024x1536, feature-graphic 1536x1024), RGB with no alpha, opaque, no borders. Contact sheets in this folder: a_lore_grey, b_display300 (300x450 with caption band and close button overlay, plus 150x225), c_vs_accepted, z_* detail crops.

Cross-cutting finding: the accepted key-art is smooth transparent watercolour. lore-1, lore-6 and feature-graphic carry a strong faceted oil-paint/Kuwahara mosaic texture across sky and nebula (scalloped 6-12 px patches, visible at 300x450), which the brief and bible class as an AI tell and which is plainly another hand than key-art. lore-2/3 have a milder scalloped texture on brass and stone that reads as aged material and is acceptable.

## lore-1 REJECTED
- Faceted mosaic texture over sky and slates, clearly noisier than accepted key-art (rubric 3, 7).
- Foreground is a flat flagstone terrace with parapet, not the briefed sloped slate roof (8).
- Key lies beside the lifted tile instead of half under it; envelope reads fine (no writing).
- Good: dust-sheet focal point and tripod read instantly, one tiny warm window, calm caption band, no people.

## lore-2 ACCEPTED
- Orrery matches instrument-orrery (three rings, three planets, crank, gear base). Lamp with believable warm glow on a blank open logbook, riveted brass dome ribs, gears mesh, no text. Gold-dominant, caption band dark and calm. Hammered look on the dome is within aged-brass tolerance; reads well at 300x450.

## lore-3 ACCEPTED
- Clean one-point perspective, ribbed vault, two hanging lamps, ladder, blank rolls and blank drawer plates, table in foreground. Amber-parchment against navy shadow, distinct from siblings. No text, no clutter in the aisle, calm lower band.

## lore-4 ACCEPTED
- Thin teal aurora with faint green and rose edge, dark sky, sparse returning stars, small silhouetted observatory with one warm window, pines. Smooth wash handling consistent with key-art. Stars are tiny and irregular; caption band calm.

## lore-5 ACCEPTED
- Clean thin gold ring on a near-black sky, no spikes, at most 4 dim stars, faint gold horizon, tiny faceless coat-and-hat figure from behind with a lamp (about 8 percent of canvas height), observatory silhouette matches the fixed design. Figure sits at the top of the caption band exactly as the brief specified; lower half is calm.

## lore-6 REJECTED
- Strongest mosaic/oil-filter texture in the batch over sky, clouds, hills, water and paving; clouds read as pixel noise at 300x450 and clash with key-art (3, 7).
- Too much distant-village and ridge detail competing with the focal key. Hood has a visible cheek/nose line (faceless rule).
- Good: lightest of the set, key glint and two small silhouettes read, eight-ray star matches icon, composition.

## feature-graphic REJECTED
- Mosaic/tessellated nebula and sky, unlike key-art's soft washes (3, 7).
- Rose nebula too saturated directly behind the building; the observatory spans x 60-87 percent, wider than the 66-86 percent third.
- The single brighter gold star is a tiny flat dot, not a readable focal star.
- Good: building design faithful to accepted observatory-building, left 55 percent calm, no text, moon or aurora.
