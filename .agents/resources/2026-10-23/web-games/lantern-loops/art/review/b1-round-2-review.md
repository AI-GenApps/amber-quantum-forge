# Lantern Loops b1 round 2 review

Result: 0 accepted, 4 rejected. No accepted assets exist yet, so there was no accepted set to compare against; consistency was checked between the four deliveries. Contact sheets in this folder: lantern-5bg.png (5 backgrounds), small-sizes.png (48/96/112 px, icon 48/96 and circular mask), display-390.png (key-art and backdrop at 390 px with safe-zone overlays), z-*.png (zooms).

Technical: all four pass. Sizes exact, lantern is RGBA with corners and outer 4 percent alpha 0 (5.6k semi-transparent pixels only on the contour), opaque assets are RGB. No text found, no checkerboard, no halo seen on night/indigo/paper/teal/grey.

## lantern-amber: REJECTED
- Cord is still a rigid vertical pin with a ring on top (flat grey-plum rod, ring of constant thickness). It reads as a metal hook, not a hanging cord with a loop. Round 1 asked for a flexible cord strip; only the width changed.
- Caps are rendered as 3D discs: the top and bottom faces are visible as ellipses (perspective tilt), with a tan metallic-looking rim that sweeps left to right like a brushed edge. Bible section 7 requires straight-on orthographic elevation and section 4 forbids metallic/3D looks. The caps need to be flat rectangular lacquer sheets with a thin top-left paper-2 edge.
- Body is smooth airbrushed gradient (soft vertical glow, soft shaded outer panels) rather than stepped flat translucent planes (section 4, 6, 7). Body is a near-sphere (54 percent wide x 50 percent tall) rather than a barrel slightly wider at the middle with a flat, clearly straight-sided profile; the two outer panels read as a ball shell.
- Good and to keep: perfect circle window, five panels / four ribs, tassel (bead + 6 stepped strips), alpha, layout proportions, colours.

## icon: REJECTED
- Hero lantern is the rejected anchor (pin cord, ellipse caps, near-sphere ball body); it must be re-rendered from an accepted anchor.
- Outer disc is a clearly more saturated, brighter blue (about #283688 / #273482) than the palette indigo-light #2B2F78 family and the saturated-only-in-lanterns rule; in the 48 px sheet it competes with the lantern. Plum disc reads brownish (#402D3C at the top), not plum #3A2A5E.
- Good and to keep: composition, tassel clearance (about 79 px), wave band with visible paper edge, full bleed, 48/96 px read, circular mask safe.

## key-art: REJECTED
- Hero and six small lanterns inherit the same near-sphere, 3D-ellipse-cap construction and metal-pin cord/ring as the rejected anchor.
- Composition flaw: the main post stands at x about 67 percent, not at the centre; the hero hangs left of it from a long arm, so the picture is lopsided, and the right-hand small lantern (x about 675) hangs directly against the post edge and overlaps it (tangent and unclear layering). The brief wanted a post at the end of a short pier rising lower-middle; there is no pier, the post just stands in the water and ends on the dark foreground sheet.
- The hero is only about 22 percent of the width and the scene is sparse and static; it does not yet look like a premium cover (shoreline gap of plain strip in the middle, tiny boats).
- Good and to keep: title band (calm, empty, no mountains), button band, flat lacquer posts with paper-2 edges, thin catenary cords, amber reflection bars, flat cut-paper water, roofs and tiny lamps, boats with one sail.

## backdrop-harbor: REJECTED
- The bottom-corner "rope coils" are single elliptical rings with bevelled, shaded, three-dimensional band geometry that read as a metal washer or key bow clamped to the post, not as flat stepped paper-strip loops. The posts and rings are the only objects in the bottom band and they look like keyholes; the construction is wrong and the style drifts to 3D.
- The bottom sheets are brownish-plum (about #231A30 against indigo) rather than near-black indigo/lacquer; tolerable but the transition reads muddy. Keep them darker and cooler.
- Good and to keep: shore (flat roofs, masts, exactly 3 tiny lamps above y 246), calm board zone (mean luminance about 12 percent), three still-water sheets with subtle paper edges, faint reflection strips, texture.
