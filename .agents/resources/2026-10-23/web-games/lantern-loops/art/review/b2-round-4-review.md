# Lantern Loops b2 round 4 review (final round)

Contact sheets: a-*-full.png (full size on grey), b-d-empty-sizes-bg.png (320 and 160 px on dark, paper, teal, grey), b-feature-1024x500.png (store crop), b-feature-small.png, c-consistency.png (against accepted lantern-unlit, lantern-amber, daily-medallion, icon), z-*.png (zoom crops).

Technical: empty-archive is 1024x1024 RGBA, corners alpha 0, outer 4 percent border alpha 0, about 3.3k semi-transparent edge pixels only. feature-graphic is 1536x1024 opaque RGB, no alpha. Both pass technically; the verdicts are art verdicts.

## empty-archive: REJECTED
- Rubric 3 / 2: the lantern is still the squat, bulbous pumpkin that was called out in rounds 2 and 3. It reads wider and rounder than the accepted lantern-unlit in c-consistency.png. The caps also carry a small cream corner flag (top-left of the top cap, bottom-left of the bottom cap) that is a construction artefact, not part of the accepted sprite.
- Rubric 2 / 8: the ribbon is a shepherd's crook / hook: a flat straight tail that starts mid-floor and ends in a single curl whose inner end nearly touches the belly. It reads as a hook or a snail tail, not a loop still to be drawn. The tail also lies over the indigo floor band without being tucked behind the front wall in any convincing way, and the 14 to 16 px ribbon thickness is not respected consistently.
- Rubric 4 / 6: at 160 px the group is a lantern on a block with a stray squiggle; the tray front wall is a huge dead cream slab (about 60 percent of the picture mass), the story (an unfinished loop waiting) is lost. Composition is left-heavy with the ribbon crammed at the right rim.
- Rubric 7: the tray is a featureless slab, not convincing folded paper; the thin indigo band is the only structure.
Verdict: reject. Final round, so a placeholder takes this slot.

## feature-graphic: REJECTED
What works: palette now matches accepted backdrop-bridge (median RGB 23,26,78 vs 19,22,70); no peach or neon; three arches, harbor houses with two amber windows, rectangular dock lamp, boat; the five lanterns are the accepted designs in the right order with varied heights; no text, moon, sparkle or glow; the 1024x500 crop keeps everything essential.
Why it still fails:
- Rubric 6 / 7 / 8: the ribbon is the hero shape and it is weak. It is a hollow, flat sausage with a large empty interior floating in the sky, the lower run is a regular sine wave and the left end bulges into a thick bend while the top run thins to a hairline, so the width is not constant. At store size it reads as a speech bubble or a wobbly paperclip with five lanterns dangling under it, not as "a luminous paper ribbon forming a loop that the lanterns light". No visible stacked paper construction, no shade plane, no light spill onto the ribbon, so the brief's "light finding its loop" idea is not communicated.
- Rubric 3: the ribbon lacks the layered cut-paper depth of the accepted assets; it looks pasted over the scene (its left curve overlaps a cloud tip and the shadow reads as an airbrushed smudge).
- Rubric 6: the five reflection groups sit under the boat, bridge arches and shore, not under the lantern x-positions, so the lantern/reflection story is disconnected. The upper 30 percent of the canvas is a flat empty navy field; the focal group floats in the middle with little connection to the scene layers.
- Rubric 4: lantern bottoms and tassels sit close to the bridge parapet line, tangling silhouettes at the crop.
Verdict: reject. A placeholder (or a simple hand-built store graphic from accepted assets) is preferable to shipping this as the Play feature graphic.
