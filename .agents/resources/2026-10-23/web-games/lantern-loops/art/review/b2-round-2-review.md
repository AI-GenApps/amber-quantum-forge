# Review: Lantern Loops b2 round 2

Technical: backdrop-sky 1024x1536 RGB opaque; daily-medallion 1024x1024 RGBA, corners alpha 0, bbox 72..952 (86 percent, 7 percent clear border), no clipped petals; empty-archive 1024x1024 RGBA, corners alpha 0, bbox 154..870 x 252..806 (inside 78 percent box, nothing in top 15 percent); feature-graphic 1536x1024 RGB opaque. Contact sheets in this folder (a-full-grey, b-display, c-backdrops-390, c-consistency, c-feature-crop, d-dark, d-light, zoom-*).

## backdrop-sky - ACCEPTED
- Round-1 faults fixed: the airbrushed rose gradient is now one flat dusty-rose paper sheet with a crisp cut edge; the sky reads violet, indigo-mist, clearly blue azure, thin rose, darker indigo as stepped sheets. At 390 px it is bluer and lighter than backdrop-bridge and clearly a different chapter; paper texture, thin edges and down-right ink shadows match backdrop-garden.
- Board zone is calm (band steps are soft, no lantern, no bright cloud edge); HUD and control bands are mid-dark; ten small lanterns in the top-left and bottom-right clusters, all under 6 percent of the width, no text, no sparkle.
- Minor (not blocking): the rose band sits inside the board zone at about 61 to 66 percent; it is low contrast and the code scrim will mute it. Tiny lantern tassels are tinted to their lantern colour rather than amber; invisible at display size.

## daily-medallion - ACCEPTED
- All 16 petals are complete, equal, fully rounded and evenly spaced; no flat clipped tips; transparent margin all round; ring, 16 ivory-core windows with thin amber-deep edge (same circle language as the amber lantern window), empty indigo centre plate (about 46 percent) with only paper texture, ready for the numeral. Clean alpha on both dark and light grounds, no fringe, no outer shadow.
- Reads as an amber and cream medal with dark empty centre at 120 px; windows crisp at 240 px. Palette, texture and edge weight match the accepted amber lantern and icon.
- Minor (not blocking): petal shading is a rotated copy so the dark facet pinwheels around the rim, which the round-2 brief itself asked for; it is not noticeable at 120/240 px. Slight pixel roughness on a few petal edges at 2x zoom only.

## empty-archive - REJECTED
- The ribbon does not read as a ribbon: about 30 px thick (board ribbon is 14 to 16 px) with ball-shaped ends on a stiff symmetric U, it reads as a horseshoe or handle at 160 px (rubric 8, purpose; rubric 3, scale rules).
- The tray is a featureless cream slab with no paper-2 shade or folded rim, and the ribbon is jammed against the right end while the left is dominated by the lantern; composition is unbalanced and the empty tray area is dead.
- The lantern is squatter and more pumpkin-like than accepted `lantern-unlit` (rounder body, no amber tassel, different loop weight) so it does not look like the same object.
- Good and to be kept: front-elevation tray with the lantern seated inside it, no trapezoid, clean alpha, nothing in the top 15 percent.

## feature-graphic - REJECTED
- The ribbon is still a paperclip: a 1010 px wide constant-width stadium that dominates the picture, with a bright cream rim glowing along its top edge (reads as outer glow, forbidden), a huge empty interior, and no stacked paper construction (rubric 7, rubric 3). It looks pasted over the scene.
- The lanterns are pasted on: no layer shadow onto the background; the amber lantern covers the house roof and window, the jade tassel touches the bridge parapet, the violet tassel touches the bush layer, so silhouettes tangle (rubric 2, 6).
- The left "harbor" and the right bank read as Garden shrubs and leaf sprigs; the harbor identity (quay, houses) is weak and the chapter-echo idea is lost.
- Good and to be kept: layered scene quality, three-arch bridge, boat with rectangular lamp, coloured reflection bars, stepped azure sky and clouds, no moon or border blob, focal group survives the 1024x500 crop (see c-feature-crop).
