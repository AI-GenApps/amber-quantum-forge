# Lantern Loops b1 round 3 review

Contact sheets and crops in this folder: a-full-grey, b-display-small (48/96/112 lantern, 48/96 icon, circle and squircle masks), b-display-390-overlays (key-art safe bands, backdrop board zone), d-lantern-four-bgs, z-* zoom crops.

Technical: all four files present, correct sizes (1024x1024 RGBA lantern; 1024x1024 icon, 1024x1536 key-art and backdrop as RGB, no alpha). Lantern alpha: corners and outer 4 percent border = 0, no semi-transparent pixels in the interior, one connected object (680 stray pixels at alpha 9 or less, invisible), no fringe on night, indigo, paper or teal.

## lantern-amber - ACCEPTED
- Round 2 faults fixed: caps are flat front-elevation lacquer rectangles with the paper-2 rim on the top-left only; hanger is a flat lacquer strip with a small folded loop (slightly spindly, but reads as paper, not a metal ring); body is stepped flat planes (deep, mid, light core) with thin paper edges and fibre texture; five panels and four ribs legible; barrel read is correct.
- Window is a clean circle; no text, no face, no halo; silhouette strong; reads as "amber lantern with a bright dot" at 48 px on night and on paper.
- Minor notes for downstream use (not blocking): loop is small and thin; tassel bead is a dark disc. Keep both unchanged in all other lanterns.

## icon - REJECTED
- Wave band is about #067A7E, far brighter and more saturated than the specified teal #0E5A5F (about 40 percent more luminous). It is the second loudest mass and competes with the lantern at 48 px and under circle masks; the bible says saturation belongs to the lanterns.
- Wave is a single dead-flat sheet: no visible paper edge, barely any layer shadow, unlike every other sheet in the set (discs, caps, lantern).
- Positives (keep): lantern identical to accepted anchor, outer disc #272E77 and plum #3D285D now correct, clean full-bleed, no text, strong 48 px read.

## key-art - REJECTED
- Lantern construction is now correct (matches the anchor), but the composition fails rubric 2, 7 and 8: the six small lanterns hang in two straight vertical columns, each dangling from the one above on a long string, so the "garland" reads as chains, not lanterns strung on a rope.
- Left and right sides are near mirror clones (same sizes and positions) and the inner small lanterns sit about 1.5 percent of the width from the hero, tangent-feeling, against the 4 percent air rule.
- The main post runs through the hero's cap, bead and tassel, so the lantern looks impaled on the post.
- Pier is three stacked brownish-plum rectangles that read as a stepped tower or ziggurat, brighter than the water and taller than the post it carries.
- Boats are two floating sail triangles with hairline hulls at identical height, half hidden by the outer posts.
- Water bands are more saturated blue (about #28308A) than indigo/indigo-light.
- Positives (keep): calm empty title band, calm dark button band, amber reflection bars, lantern consistency.

## backdrop-harbor - REJECTED
- Shore, board zone and bottom sheets are right: board zone mean luminance about 13 percent, band steps about 5 percent (under 8), no detail in the board zone, cooler darker bottom sheets as requested.
- The only fix requested (mooring posts and coils) is half done: each of the three coil bars and the post top carries a continuous bright paper-2 line along the whole top edge, i.e. exactly the "tan full-width stroke" the brief forbade. It reads as a metallic rim and the stack reads like a game-piece tower, not a rope coil.
- Posts and bars are mauve (about #362241), lighter and warmer than the near-black sheet behind them, so they are the brightest objects in the bottom band instead of low-contrast lacquer (#2B1F3A) and would fight the buttons.
- Fix is tiny and targeted; everything else must stay.
