## icon: ACCEPTED
- Technical: 1024x1024 RGB, no alpha, full-bleed, no border, mask or baked rounded corners.
- Lantern is the accepted lantern-amber construction unchanged (flat lacquer caps with left-half rim, stepped panels, circle window, flat tassel); same edge, texture and palette. Compared side by side, it is the same object.
- Round-3 defect fixed: wave sampled at about RGB 16,92,104 (target #0E5A5F), so it is muted mid-dark teal, no longer a loud cyan band. It is now built from two stacked sheets with a thin lighter top-left cut edge and a soft down-right shadow. Tassel clearance is clear.
- Readability: at 48 and 96 px, and under circle and rounded-square masks, it reads instantly as a glowing lantern on dark discs. The lantern is the brightest, most saturated mass; the wave is a quiet secondary band.
- No text, no extra objects, no sparkles, no glow bloom, no gloss.
- Minor, not blocking: no warm spill on the nearest disc edge (brief said one is optional in effect); the wave cyan edge is slightly crisper than the lantern rim.

## backdrop-harbor: REJECTED
- Brief asked for the two mooring posts to be cleaned: dim, partial rims, lacquer colour, readable as a mooring post, or posts with no coil stacks if it could not be done cleanly.
- Zoom (z-backdrop-posts.png): the rim is still a bright cream-tan line (max about RGB 211,188,167, essentially the original paper-2 and not the requested 40 percent dimmer) on every post cap and on all three bars, running round the left end and down the left corners. These stay the brightest, most contrasty items in the dark bottom band at 390 px (b-backdrop-390-overlays.png), competing with the control band.
- The posts still read as a stacked game piece (a column on a three-disc stepped base, like a Hanoi tower or chess piece), not a mooring post. Face colour (about 38,26,53) is also slightly lighter and warmer than the near-black sheet. The brief's own fallback (post only, no stacks and no rims) was not taken.
- The rest (shore, three calm water sheets, mean luminance about 12 percent in the board zone, near-black bottom sheets, texture) is fine but extremely sparse and flat for the most-seen backdrop; the only nautical content is the two posts, and these fail. Because the bible makes a post-fault a style and readability failure, and this is the final round, it is not accepted.
