Contact sheets in this folder: a_full_grey, b_display, c_consistency, d_alpha_bgs, e_sky_crop, e_cobble_crop.

## key-art: REJECTED
Technical: 1024x1536 RGB, no alpha; title band mean about (29,52,88), contrast fine. Roof peak at about y 30 percent, building within x 27 to 71. Technically OK.
Reasons (rubric 3, 6, 7):
1. The repeated scale/fish-scale texture the brief told the designer to remove is still everywhere: the sky is a tessellation of identical scalloped cells in diagonal bands, the cobbles a uniform hex mosaic. It reads as a filter, not gouache, and does not match the irregular mottled dry-brush of accepted/interior-evening.png.
2. The sky is a loud orange/pink striped sunset over the whole right side, not a calm dusk wash with a thin warm band; it competes with the window glow and the title band is textured with strong diagonal strokes.
3. The house still reads as a toy/doll house (small, machine-ruled clapboard, tiny arch); style differs from the painterly interior.
4. Window focal point sits at y 63 percent, far from the brief's y 50; the bottom 40 percent is a huge, evenly textured cobble field.
Good: two storeys, sea wall continuous, bollard, boats, wall lamp, sage awning, lamp/books/cup match the icon, no text/people/rain.

1. Face is still a smooth 3D-animation-style render: glossy eyes with catchlights, blended pink cheeks, porcelain skin. Not matte brushy gouache like the accepted interior and icon; looks from another set.
2. Hair is ropey blue-and-white stripes like wool yarn; the bun looks like a ball of yarn.
3. Cardigan is saturated royal blue with a mechanically repeated braid, not navy #26384F/#2D4560; horn buttons are bright orange.
