# Review: batch b7, round 1

Technical: all four files present, exact sizes (1024x1024 RGBA x3, 1536x1024 RGB opaque). Decor corners and outer 31 px border alpha 0. Sheets: a_full_grey, b_display, c_with_accepted, d_dark_light, e_fg_band, plus zoom crops z_*.

## decor-6 - ACCEPTED
- Frontal frame, dark wood with lit top-left bevel, cream mat, sepia coast, blue-green sea, wavy contours, 8-point compass with no letters, dotted route, small anchor. No text. Frame 80 percent wide / 62 percent tall, soft down-right shadow under 30 percent alpha.
- Matches accepted decor-1..4 in pigment handling, edge and palette. Reads instantly as a framed sea chart at 48 and 96 px.
- Minor, tolerated: the land blotches have slightly ragged edges and the brown coast rim is a little worm-like at 2x zoom; invisible at display size.

## decor-7 - REJECTED
- Construction: the central cord and bead clapper are painted in FRONT of the centre tube (cord enters a hole at the tube top then runs down the outside of the opaque tube, bead overlaps the tube body). It reads as a pierced tube and as a construction error rather than a clapper hanging inside.
- Brass is a flat, over-saturated yellow-gold with almost no value steps and no cool shade side; accepted brass (decor-2 lamp, key-art lantern) has lit / mid / deep steps and a cool shade. Tubes look like flat yellow rectangles.
- Silhouette, symmetry (5 tubes, short/medium/long/medium/short), width 41 percent, height 86 percent and the opaque blue-grey drop are fine and should be kept.

## decor-8 - REJECTED
- Rendering technique mismatch: the horn, cabinet and stool carry a visible square mosaic / tile pattern (a blocky digital texture), not gouache brush strokes. It is clearly visible at 300 px and at the 128 css px shop card size, and reads as a CG artefact next to the brushy accepted lamp, cushions and cake stand (rubric 3 and 7).
- Horn gradients read as faceted CG metal planes rather than two or three painted value fields.
- Geometry is otherwise good (clean oval mouth, connected neck, crank, plain red record centre, three stool legs, soft contact shadow) and should be kept.

## feature-graphic - ACCEPTED
- Same impasto/gouache handling, palette and architecture as accepted key-art: clapboard shop with big round-arched window and transom, sage awning, porthole door, lit wall lamp, glowing window lamp as the brightest point (measured peak 251 vs lanterns about 200). Eight paper-box lanterns with dark bases and broken reflections, two moored boats, far shore lights, warm horizon band. No text, people, birds, halos, vignette or frame.
- Essential content sits inside the y 16 to 84 percent centre band (checked in e_fg_band). Horizon sits around 52 percent rather than 58, harmless. Lantern warm tones are a little uniform; acceptable.
