# Review: b5 round 2 (drink-6)

## drink-6 - ACCEPTED

Technical: 1024x1024 RGBA, all corners and the outer 3 percent border alpha 0; mug body opaque; one soft ink contact shadow down-right inside the canvas; clean edges on night, nightMid, paper and wood backgrounds (contact sheet d). Contents span about y187-930, about 71 percent of height including the shadow.

Round-1 defect fixed: the diamond/lattice tiling is gone. The body is now a few long, soft vertical dry-brush passes (lit left band, narrow highlight strip, darker right shade), matching the stroke scale of the accepted drink-5/drink-1. The handle shares the same paint softness and texture scale. Verified in the full-size view and at 2x zoom of the rim and handle.

Rubric: no text, motifs, steam or extra objects. Brass band is a clean ring following the rim ellipse. Foam dome with four cocoa flecks and a dark mocha edge. Single right handle attached at both ends with a real hole. Light from upper left, same pitch as siblings. At 32/48/96 px (sheet b) it reads as a tall navy mug with gold band and foam, clearly distinct from the short cream mug, glass tumbler and red mug (sheet c). Palette matches the brief hexes. Minor: the dark attach shading where the handle meets the body is slightly heavy at zoom, invisible at display size; the foam is a touch smoother than the faceted older mugs, within family tolerance.
