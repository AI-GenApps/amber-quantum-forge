# Review: The Last Window, batch b7, round 2

Technical (both): 1024x1024 RGBA, corners alpha 0, outer 3% border alpha 0, no pure black/white pixels, no baked checkerboard, clean edges on night / nightMid / paper / wood. decor-7 has no contact shadow (correct, hangs); decor-8 has one soft ink-blue shadow, inside margin.

## decor-7 (Rain Chime): ACCEPTED
- The round-1 construction fault is fixed: the central cord is hidden behind the centre tube, emerges below its opening, the wooden bead sits entirely below the tube and above the drop, no overlap. Five symmetric graduated tubes, each on its own cord; ring, chain, disc, opaque pale blue-grey drop with one paper highlight. No text, no rain, no ground.
- Brass now has a lit left edge, mid body and darker right side with lengthwise dry-brush strokes, saturation matched to accepted decor-2 (about 0.69 vs 0.69). Consistent with decor-2/3 in the side-by-side sheets.
- Minor, not player-visible at 128 px: the cool far-right shade is weak, tubes are fairly uniform yellow, the chain links are slightly glossy-3D, and there is faint stair-stepping on the tube right edges at 200%. Reads clearly at 96 and 48 px as a brass chime with a blue drop; silhouette is distinct from siblings.

## decor-8 (Brass Gramophone): ACCEPTED
- Round-1 faults fixed: no square mosaic or grid. At 200% the horn shows soft irregular dabbed patches (no tiles, no rectangles); cabinet and stool read as broad wood fields with faint grain. The horn has lit ochre, mid brass and a cool ink-blue shade in the bell, one clean smooth oval mouth, a collar and curved pipe connecting to a brass mount on the cabinet.
- Cabinet, plain brick-red record centre (no label text), short tonearm, right crank with wooden handle, exactly three stool legs (the back one foreshortened), soft down-right contact shadow. Light from upper left, matches the accepted set. Strongest silhouette in the decor set; reads at 48 px.
- Minor, accepted: the horn texture is a little rougher and more painterly than the lamp, with faintly polygonal patches inside the bell at 200%; the cabinet base slightly overhangs the stool on the right. Neither is visible at 96-256 px.
