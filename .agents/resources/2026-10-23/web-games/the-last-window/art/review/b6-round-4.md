# Review b6 round 4 (final round)

## decor-5: REJECTED

Technical (pass): 1024x1024 RGBA PNG, corners and 3 percent border alpha 0, bbox 302,120 to 728,971 (about 83 percent tall, 42 percent wide), centred. Shadow is a thin light grey-blue ellipse. No checkerboard or halo seen on the four-background sheet.

Rejected for:
1. Rendering technique (rubric 3, 7): it still reads as a smooth, clean 3D/CG render, not matte gouache. Rails, rungs and foot caps are hard-edged bevelled boxes with crisp vector-straight edges and smooth gradients. The brass spheres and caps have a shaded metal-ball look. Next to decor-2/3/4 (visible dry-brush, paper tooth, soft edges), it looks like it came from another set. The only texture is a faint diagonal scratch overlay on the rails, which is the grain-filter look the brief banned.
2. View (rubric 2, 3): the ladder is almost a flat frontal elevation. There is no visible 8 degree lean-back and no 30 degree downward view. Rung top strips are barely visible, and the rail tops are flat cut faces. The foot caps are tiny cuboids. It does not share the pitch of the accepted floor decor.
3. Palette (rubric 3, 7): the wood is a saturated orange, hotter than the #8A5A36 / #B8794A family. The shade planes are a plain brown, not ink-blue mixed. The brass is a flat yellow-orange blob with little value structure.
4. Construction (rubric 2): the "wheels" are plain ball joints on a short stub, not wheel-and-bracket hooks. The rod passes through the balls and extends beyond them.
5. Shadow: thin, light grey-blue and tight under the feet, close to the brief, but it does not rescue the style mismatch.

Readability at 32/48/96 px is fine. Silhouette and six rungs read clearly. The failure is style consistency with the accepted set, so the asset is rejected. This is the final round, so the slot stays open for a placeholder. No entry was added to accepted/manifest.json and nothing was copied to accepted/.
