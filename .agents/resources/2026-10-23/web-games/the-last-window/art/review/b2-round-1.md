# Review - The Last Window, batch b2, round 1

Technical checks (PIL): all 9 files present, correct sizes (interiors 1024x1536 RGB, no alpha; busts 1020x1360 RGBA), bust corners and outer 3 percent border alpha 0, shoulders within 6 to 94 percent, no checkerboard or fringe on night/paper composites. Interior luminance: morning 49 percent (limit 35 to 50, at the edge), rain 26 percent (25 to 38).
Contact sheets: a-*, b-*, c-*, d-* in this folder.

Verdicts: ACCEPTED 6 (customer-2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8 neutral). REJECTED 3 (interior-morning, interior-rain, customer-5-neutral).

## interior-morning - REJECTED
Geometry, window view, unlit lamp, bookcase, stand and counter all match the evening. Strong asset otherwise. Fails: broad diagonal light shafts from the upper left cross the reserved left wall, the UI zone (x 24 to 76, y 42 to 78) and the counter. These are god rays (hard-fail item 7), break the calm-zone rule, and are physically inconsistent (window on same wall). White foam patches at the window base read as sea spray, not steam. Luminance 49 percent is at the ceiling.

## interior-rain - REJECTED
Geometry and lit lamp match. Fails: outside view is still the evening dusk (deep blue, pink/orange horizon band, amber glints on water) instead of harbour-grey/rain; round pale dots over the sea are bokeh; streaks are a few crisp white lines; the umbrella is a tiny crumpled clump that does not read, base puddle sheen barely there; the room is nearly indistinguishable from the evening (purpose/distinctness).
