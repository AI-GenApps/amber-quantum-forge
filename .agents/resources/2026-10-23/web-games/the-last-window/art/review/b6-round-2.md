# The Last Window - b6 round 2 review

Technical: all five files are 1024x1024 RGBA, corners and outer 3 percent border alpha 0, contact shadow semi-transparent only, fill 74-90 percent. Manifest valid, ids match. Contact sheets in this folder (a-full, d-dark, d-light, c-books, c-decor, b-192, b-48/96 dark/light).

## book-2 - ACCEPTED
Lattice gone; cover painted in 2-3 broad vertical passes, lighter blue-grey as briefed. Two sparrows upper right (simple, correct anatomy), three cream waves in the lower third, cream spine bands, slim spine, same pose/pitch/shadow as book-1/book-3. Reads at 48 px (blue book, waves, birds). No text.

## book-4 - ACCEPTED
Lattice gone, calm yellow body (about #EDBE3C, lit #F4C848) consistent with the set. One inside-out teal umbrella with 5-6 ribs, one crook handle, two cream motion strokes; distinct silhouette from siblings at 48 px. Rib-tip beads are small and acceptable. Slightly brighter than the dark leather books but within palette and distinct by design.

## book-5 - ACCEPTED
Pale sky blue cover, cream border line, cream spine with two red bands. Gull is now anatomically simple: one body, one folded grey-blue wing, two legs with webbed feet, one eye, orange beak; red hat with cream band sits on the head. Squarish picture-book proportion, same pose and pitch as the other books. Reads clearly at 48 px.

## decor-3 - REJECTED
- Foliage is a mound of ball-shaped fluffy clumps rendered with a directional smudge/oil-filter look (jagged stair-stepped smears); it reads as a topiary or juniper shrub, not rosemary. Brief asked for 10 to 14 upright sprigs radiating upward with needle-leaf clumps.
- Brush texture does not match the accepted gouache objects (decor-1, pastry-1): it is noisier and more mechanical.
- Pot body shows hard vertical facet seams (flat panels) rather than 2-3 soft passes following the form; rim band is thin, not the thick band specified.
- Only 3 flowers, all tiny and identical-looking.
Pot colour, base position, soil, shadow and size are fine and should be kept.

## decor-5 - REJECTED
- Rendering is glossy CG (specular on brass, noisy wood-grain filter), not matte gouache; clearly from another set than accepted decor-1/2/4 and the drinks.
- Construction: the brass hooks/wheels sit on top of the rail tops and the horizontal brass rail floats above them; wheels are not hooked onto or running on the rail. Wheel discs are flat coins stuck to the stem.
- Ladder is a straight front elevation with no 30 degree pitch or backward lean; rails look flat, no sense of standing; contact shadow is a faint grey line, not the standard night-ink soft shadow.
- Palette: wood and brass are too saturated and orange against the cool-shade family.
