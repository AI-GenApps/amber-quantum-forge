## feature-graphic: REJECTED (gloss still present on stamp, lens and lamp; crackle on blotter)
Good and kept: composition, reading order, safe band (crop loses nothing), five objects distinct at 300 px, no text/people/animals, plain drawers, no chain, no mesh on bars, two flat pool discs, flat window and teal/mustard colour now close to accepted tin and compass, grain is much closer to the accepted sprites, lamp leaf is now a mostly flat shape. Big improvement overall.

Why it still fails (all visible at 1024x500 or at the 2x zoom):
1. STAMP KNOB is still glossy: a bright near-white, curved specular stroke (not a small flat cream crescent) with a soft light edge, and two dark round brick-deep blobs on the right side and neck that are soft-edged smudges, not a 36 px dot halftone. Reads as a glossy plastic bulb. This was item 1 of the round-3 brief and is not fixed; the manifest claim of a flat knob is wrong.
2. MAGNIFIER LENS highlight is a glassy white-to-teal soft band with a feathered inner edge, not a hard flat cream crescent. The three lower-right shade dots are three blobs of different size and softness, not a coarse regular dot system. The brass handle carries a thin pale specular streak (polished metal), unlike the matte compass and key brass.
3. LAMP: the mustard-light leaf is acceptable in shape, but it has a bright cream rim line down its edge with a feathered inner fade, and faint crack-like veins inside it. The bulb disc is near-white with a gradient edge instead of a flat cream disc. The joint-knob at the shade top and the lower joint carry small white specular pips.
4. BLOTTER: a cobblestone/crackle network (polygon cell outlines) sits on the lower-left teal and a net-like halftone on the lower-right, which is the "polygonal crackle" the generator itself said it had removed. This also shows faintly on the parcel bottom and the counter front. Not a dot halftone, not paper-tooth speckle; it reads as a digital texture overlay.
5. Cast shadows under the objects (compass, key, hat box, tin, cloth, stamp, magnifier) are a fine diagonal mesh, not the coarse 36 px round-dot field used everywhere else; fine-pitch halftone turns to moire at 300 px.

Verdict: much closer, but specular gloss on the stamp, lens and lamp is exactly what the accepted sprites forbid (rubric 3 and 7). Reject; round 4 brief written. Nothing accepted this round.
