# b1 round 1 review

Contact sheets: a-full-grey.png, b-display.png (icon 48/96/192 + round mask, postcard 54/84/220 on night and cream), b2-scenes390.png (key-art and backdrop at 390 px with title/button and header/bottom bands), d-transparent-bgs.png (postcard on night, cream, teal-deep, mustard), z-*.png (zoom crops). No accepted assets existed before this round, so no consistency sheet (c) was possible; the postcard becomes the anchor.

Technical: icon 1024x1024 RGB; key-art 1024x1536 RGB; desk-backdrop 1024x1536 RGB; postcard 1024x1024 RGBA, corners alpha 0, outer 6 percent border clear, bbox 78.0 percent wide, only silhouette antialias is semi-transparent. No pure black or white pixels anywhere.

## evidence-postcard: ACCEPTED
Clean keyline, coarse round-dot sky and shadow at the bible pitch, visible grain and cream-gap misregistration, correct palette, no text, no halo on any of the four test backgrounds. Reads at 54 and 84 px as a sunny seaside card with brick pier and striped hut. Minor: sand stipple dots are slightly busy but invisible at display size. Becomes the style anchor.

## icon: REJECTED
- The brick "scalloped square stamp" is a shapeless jagged red blob above the parcel; reads as a stain at 96 px.
- Composition is lopsided toward upper-left with an empty lower-right of the disc; magnifier handle collides with the rings.
- Parcel is a flat front rectangle; brief asked for top face plus thin front side.
- Disc outline is wobbly rather than a true circle.
Positives: palette, keylines, coarse halftone, readable lens at 48 px.

## key-art: REJECTED
- Fine dot screen on the teal counter (about 8-10 px pitch) breaks the one-pitch rule and moires at 390 px.
- Window is an empty dark void with two lines; no river/pane read. Rain dashes float over a thin teal strip.
- Stamp is tiny and its pad vanishes; envelope stack uses a different, non top-down angle and merges into the lamp pool.
- Lamp neck and shade merge into the pigeonhole row with little separation.
- Feels like clean vector more than riso print; must also be redone against accepted icon/backdrop per the brief.
Safe zones: title band is dark and calm, button band is calm; hero inside x 15-85 percent. These are fine.

## desk-backdrop: REJECTED
- Props (bell, stamp, pencil, twine) are airbrushed with glossy highlights and no ink keyline: style break from the postcard.
- Bell carries squiggly engraved marks that read as fake lettering (hard-fail rule 1).
- Cast shadows are partly soft rather than a crisp dot field.
- Wood edges inconsistent (thin left/right, thick bottom).
Positives: calm near-neutral blotter (mean luminance 14 percent), empty centre, quiet header/bottom, pool nearly invisible.
