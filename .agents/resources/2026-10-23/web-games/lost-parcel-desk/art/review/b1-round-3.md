## key-art: REJECTED
Real progress (cubby grid is now clean and closed, window reads as one window, title band calm, parcel/magnifier/stamp share the icon look). Still failing:
- Bottom band is a loud checkerboard of big teal and night squares split by a rail, not vertical boards. It is the biggest shape in the image, under the button band, and reads as a tile floor. Explicit brief miss.
- Lamp: shade overlaps the vertical divider between cubbies and the shelf row, neck is a thick straight pole (brief: arched neck), thick cream halo instead of a 5 px misregistration sliver, no halftone shading. Does not look like the same set as the icon.
- Parcel is a skewed parallelogram (right edge slants out, top face has no consistent projection) and is not level; does not match the icon parcel construction.
- Shadow dots under the parcel and lamp base form a free-floating row across the cream pool rather than a crescent hugging each object.
- Grain/riso feel is weak in window and bottom band (flat digital fills).

## desk-backdrop: REJECTED
Positives: calm near-neutral blotter, empty centre, clean wood border, header/bottom bands calm, no text, no pool smudge.
- Props are far too small and mushy at real size: at 390 px wide each is about 30 px and reads as a blob. The stamp is an unreadable tangle of rings and stripes; the bell is a yellow disc with scattered cream specks (stray flecks, a second offset ring); the twine spool reads as a cream target with a stray tail; the pencil is a dark smear.
- Misregistration slips in inconsistent directions, giving doubled outlines on the stamp and bell.
- Shadow dots are partly missing or half-cut (pencil, spool, bell, stamp) rather than a clean 24 px dot crescent.
- Props sit very close to the wood edge on the right (pencil tip near the margin) and will be cropped on cover-fit.
Per the rubric (readability at display size, style consistency), rejected.

Accepted this round: none. Round-4 brief written.
