# b1 round 2 review

Sheets: a-full-grey.png, b-display.png (icon 48/96/192, circle mask), b2-scenes390.png (key-art and backdrop at 390 px with bands), c-consistency.png (icon, accepted postcard, key-art hero), z-* zoom crops.
Technical: icon 1024x1024 RGB, key-art 1024x1536 RGB, desk-backdrop 1024x1536 RGB. All opaque (no alpha). No border/vignette. Negligible near-black/white specks. Backdrop table-zone mean luminance 14.5 percent (in range).

## icon: ACCEPTED
All four round-1 faults fixed: stamp now a clean scalloped square with keyline, half hidden behind the parcel and reading as a stamp; group is centred by mass with the handle well clear of the rings; parcel shows top face plus front strip; disc is a true circle with two crisp rings. Reads at 48 px (cream badge, brown parcel, teal lens) and 96 px (bow, label, rim). Palette, keyline weight, coarse shadow dots, grain and up-left misregistration match the postcard anchor. No text.
Minor, accepted because not noticeable at display size: the parcel front strip uses a finer halftone than the 24 px system; parcel top edges are slightly non-parallel (mild trapezoid); lens fill has a faint tonal drift.

## key-art: REJECTED
- Cubby wall is badly constructed: shelf and divider lines stop short or are missing (stub dividers at x 400-415 and under the lamp, row 1 and row 2 not closed), the second row has a lighter rectangular band at x 210-470 that reads as a rendering error rather than lamp light. Impossible/unfinished structure.
- Lamp still merges with the pigeonholes: the shade overlaps the row line and the neck stands against cubby fills with no clear separation. This was a round-1 note and is not fixed.
- Rubber stamp: pad is dark teal on a teal counter and vanishes again; the stamp is a tiny red lump with a dot-top and is not clearly a stamp with ink pad.
- Title band is filled with white speckle dots that read as stars (must-not) and a heavy window grid that spans the whole band and continues into the button band as a tile grid; it does not read as one window onto a river night. The teal river sits in a separate boxed strip with the muntin cut through it.
- Parcel sits at a rotation that does not match the flat counter logic and the cream gap/misregistration is barely visible on lamp, parcel and magnifier; overall it reads as clean flat vector, not the riso print of the icon and postcard.
Positives: coarse dot pitch is now right, no moire, safe zones respected, hero inside x 15-85 percent, envelope stack tidy.

## desk-backdrop: REJECTED
- Props repeat the round-1 style break. The brass bell dome has a glassy faceted specular highlight and soft airbrushed gradients, a blurry second ring, and no keyline on the dome. Pencil and twine spool have no keyline and soft shading. The stamp has a soft blurred outline instead of a crisp keyline. None matches the postcard's flat fills, crisp 15 px keyline and coarse halftone.
- Cast shadows are soft, low-contrast blurred dots, not the crisp opaque ink dot field; the right-hand shadows run onto the wood edge and are clipped into odd shapes. The stamp sits over the left wood border.
- The lamp pool is a visible dirty smudge with wobbly, mottled edges in the table zone, not two crisp flat overlapping discs; it reads as a stain.
- Props are tiny (about 6-9 percent of width) and crowded into the extreme edge so they will be cropped by cover-fit.
Positives: calm near-neutral blotter, empty centre, consistent wood border (3 percent sides, 6 percent bottom), mean luminance in range, no text now.
