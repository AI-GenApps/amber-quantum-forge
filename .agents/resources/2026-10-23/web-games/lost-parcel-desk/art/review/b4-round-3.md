# Review b4 round 3 - Lost Parcel Desk

Contact sheets: a_full_bgs.png (grey/night/cream), b_display.png (54/84/220), c_siblings.png and c2_siblings84x2.png (vs postcard, 09-3, 10-1, 04-1).

## evidence-08-1 - REJECTED

Technical: PASS. 1024x1024 RGBA, corners alpha 0, outer 6 percent border clear, bbox 72.8 x 78.5 percent, no fringe on grey/night/cream. (51 minor semi-alpha interior px at antialiased inner edges; not a reason.)

Rubric failures:
1. Purpose (8) - FAIL, the deciding point. At 54/84 px it reads as a thimble / bell / beehive / road-cone hat, not a rubber walking-stick tip. The taper is a long straight cone (top about 40 percent of base width, much stronger than the 55 percent in the brief) and the body is divided into four equal horizontal tiers, so it looks like a stacked party hat. There is no distinct foot band, which is the only cue that makes a ferrule a ferrule.
2. Construction (2) - FAIL. The wear "facet" is a single straight ink line running from the second ring to the base, cutting the whole object like a seam; the lighter triangle left of it reads as a pie slice or a panel, not a worn flat on rubber. The silhouette itself is not flattened there, so it is not a facet. The lowest ring sits above the tread and duplicates the foot edge, making a fourth tier.
3. Tread (2/8) - FAIL. The five ribs are isolated dark vertical pills floating on the base band at different heights; they read as slots/windows/vents (explicitly forbidden). They are not moulded ribs and are not foreshortened along the foot ellipse in a believable way (they sit on a straight-ish line, the left two are drawn higher than the right).
4. Style (3/7) - FAIL. The highlight is a wide pale pink band with a soft sheen and a curved hard top, which reads glossy/plastic compared to the flat matte planes on evidence-09-3 and 10-1; it is also pinker than #BD6554 on the upper left. Keyline and grain are acceptable and the right-side halftone matches the postcard, but there is no cream, so the object is a single flat red mass with weak value structure.
5. Shadow (5/7) - FAIL. The cast-shadow crescent is a loose scatter: it floats at the lower left (dots at x 160-250 detached below the foot), a stray block of dots hangs under the base centre, and the right side dots stack in uneven steps. The brief required a clean crescent hugging right and bottom only.
6. Readability (4) - the silhouette is clear and strong over night and cream, but it names the wrong object.

Accepted: none. Revision brief written to b4/round-4/brief.md.
