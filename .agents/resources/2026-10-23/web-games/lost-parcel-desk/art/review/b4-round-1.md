## REJECTED
- evidence-08-1 Rubber ferrule: (1) rendered like glossy lacquer or ceramic: long cream specular streaks on the rim and left of every ring, which the brief forbids (no glossy rubber shine) and which breaks the matte riso look; (2) the "worn flat patch" is an irregular blotchy stain with a ragged edge, not a flat lighter facet; (3) the whole object is near-black maroon, so on night #141A22 the silhouette and the cast shadow nearly vanish; (4) with an open socket as a big dark hole it reads as a flower pot / thimble / cup at 84 px rather than a walking-stick ferrule.
- evidence-08-3 Cushion cover: the brief's calm hotspot area (plain mustard patch about 20 percent of the width at the upper-left, free of pattern) is missing: the teal lattice and cream dots run right into the upper-left corner. Also the lattice is cropped irregularly at the right and top edges (not a tidy 4x4 of whole diamonds) and the -4 degree rotation is barely visible.
