# Review: batch b6, round 4 (final) - Lost Parcel Desk

## townsfolk-9 (Iggy Marsh, signaller): REJECTED

Technical (pass): 960x1280 RGBA, corners and 3 percent border alpha 0, content bbox x67-892, y76-1202 (bottom cut at about 94 percent), no baked background. Manifest present, but its own notes say "PARTIAL / DRAFT: final visual self-review FAILED".

Reasons for rejection:
1. Required look feature fails (rubric 2, 4, 8). The hearing aid is the identity-defining feature. At 54x72 and 108x144 (b_display_x3.png) there is NO visible hearing aid; the right ear is just a plain ear. Cosmo (townsfolk-4) shows a clearly legible beige aid at the same sizes.
2. Casing does not read as a behind-the-ear unit (rubric 2). At 960x1280 (e_ear_right.png) it is a pale mustard halftone-filled crescent behind the ear with the same dot treatment as hair shade, no cream-2 flat fill, and no interior ink keyline. It reads as a second ear rim, a bun of hair or a patch of shade, not a device hooked over the ear top.
3. Tube and earpiece are malformed (rubric 2, 7). The tube is a thin wobbly ink hook that does not clearly join the casing; the earpiece is a small rounded tile with a dark slot/pit inside it, which reads as a stud earring or a pierced lobe. The designer acknowledged these leftover artefacts (pale slit in the tube, dark slot in the mould).
4. Style drift against accepted siblings (rubric 3). The cap carries glossy teal rim-light streaks and a lot of high-contrast halftone, the hair is wavy curls with a different line character than the flat short hair on accepted busts, and the face (large eyes with heavy lids, thin face) is a different construction from townsfolk-1/-4/-6. Side by side (c_compare.png) it looks like a different hand.
5. Left ear is mildly larger and rounder than the right ear region, so ears are not clearly level/symmetric once the aid mass is added.

What is fine: clean continuous outer keyline, correct framing numbers, matte fills, stubble patch, no text, no spectacles, no stick.

Verdict: REJECTED. This is the fourth failed delivery and the final round; the slot stays a placeholder. Nothing copied to accepted/ and accepted/manifest.json is unchanged.
