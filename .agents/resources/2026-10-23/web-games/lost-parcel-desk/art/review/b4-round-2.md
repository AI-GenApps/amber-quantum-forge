## evidence-08-1 - REJECTED
- Reads as a traffic cone / training pylon, not a rubber cane tip: tall (about 74 percent high on a 58 percent foot), pointed taper, evenly spaced rings, flared flange. A stranger at 84 px says "road cone".
- The "flat highlight facets" became small pink parallelograms stuck on the left of each ring and on the dome; they look like tape or stickers, and the glossy parallelogram on the dome still reads as lacquer.
- The wear facet is a pink translucent-looking rectangle that reads as a sticker/plaster patch, not a flattened worn side of rubber.
- Foot ridges are loose rectangular slots sitting on the flange; they look like bolts or windows rather than tread ribs.
- Positives: brick mid-tone now clearly lighter than night, no black blob, no streak highlights, keyline/dot pitch/grain match postcard.

## evidence-08-3 - ACCEPTED
- Mustard cushion with teal diamond lattice (15 whole diamonds), cream dots at crossings, cream piping with rounded corners, thin flap line near the lower edge, halftone puff on lower right only, matte fills.
- Calm hotspot patch in the upper-left verified by crop: unbroken plain mustard, no teal, no dots, no halftone, no fold.
- Style matches postcard, lens cloth and matte volumes: keyline, dot pitch, grain, top-left light, down-right dot shadow. Reads instantly as a patterned cushion at 54/84/220 px on night and cream.
- Minor, tolerated: slightly lumpy puffed piping, lattice bottom tips sit close to the flap line, stair-step lattice edge where the corner is omitted. None break style or read as errors at display size.
