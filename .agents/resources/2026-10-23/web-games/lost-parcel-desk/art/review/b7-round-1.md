# b7 round 1 review

Technical: parcel-closed and parcel-open are 1024x1024 RGBA, corners alpha 0, outer 6% border alpha 0, bounding box 78% on the long side. feature-graphic is 1536x1024 RGB, no alpha. Contact sheets in this folder (a: full size, b: display size and 1024x500 crop, c: beside accepted tin/key/hat box/cloth, d: four backgrounds, z-*: detail crops).

## parcel-closed: ACCEPTED
- No text or marks anywhere; stamps and postmark are icon-only; label rectangle is flat cream with zero dark marks inside x205-532, y287-430 (max channel drift 6 levels).
- Twine cross sits right of and below the label zone as briefed; eyelet and loop outside the clear area.
- Keyline weight, kraft grain, coarse round-dot shade and cast-shadow dot pitch match accepted tin, key and postcard side by side. Down-right light and up-left misregistration agree.
- Reads at 180 and 72 px as a tied brown parcel with label and stamps. Clean on night, cream, teal-deep and mustard, no fringe.

## parcel-open: ACCEPTED
- Reads instantly at 72 px as an open kraft box with spilled tissue; silhouette is clearly distinct from parcel-closed. Same kraft and twine as the closed parcel; box is empty; no text, label or stamps.
- Coarse dot shade on the far wall and tide band, crisp dot cast shadow, same keyline as accepted sprites. Clean on all four backgrounds.
- Minor, invisible at display size: tissue facets are a little low-poly and the cut twine hump reads slightly like a handle. Not a player-visible fault.

## feature-graphic: REJECTED (rendering style break: gloss and airbrush)
Composition, safe band, no text, no people and the five redrawn objects (compass, key, hat box, tin, cloth) are all good; the 1024x500 crop keeps every essential item and the 300 px read is clear. The problems:
1. Lamp shade and arm have smooth gradient shading and white specular streaks: a chrome/plastic gloss, forbidden (rubric 7, bible sections 4 and 6). The accepted sprites use flat steps and halftone only.
2. The bulb under the shade is a soft radial hot spot: reads as glow, which the brief forbids.
3. Rubber-stamp knob has a glassy specular dot and the magnifier lens has a gradient sheen.
4. A uniform dirty speckle/scratch overlay covers the scene (top-left envelope, blotter, wood, pool): vintage-filter look, not clean riso grain; flat fills are not flat.
5. Window, blotter and pool show smooth tonal drift instead of stepped flats.
Round 2 brief keeps composition and objects, fixes only the technique.
