## feature-graphic: REJECTED (gloss and airbrush still present; render is cleaner and smoother than the accepted set)
Kept and good: composition, reading order, safe band (the 1024x500 crop loses nothing), five objects legible as distinct items, no text, people or animals, blank parcel label, two stepped pool discs, bulb is now a crisp disc, window and blotter are flat, the generic dirty overlay is gone, the lens is flat teal-light with coarse dots.

Why it still fails:
1. Stamp knob is still glossy: a bright white-cream teardrop specular with soft edges plus a second thin specular line on the neck, and an airbrushed dark smudge on the right side of the knob (not a dot halftone). It reads as a plastic bulb. The brief asked for ONE flat cream crescent and no gloss.
2. Lamp shade highlight is a pale sheen with soft/feathered edge and tonal drift (measured std about 70 levels across the patch, with many intermediate tones), not a flat mustard-light shape; the shade and arm still read as polished metal. Accepted brass (compass, key) is matte flat steps with grain.
3. Magnifier lens carries a glassy curved white highlight plus a thin second arc, which reads as a glossy glass sphere rather than the flat cream crescent used by the accepted sprites.
4. Style gap against the accepted set (rubric 3 and 7): accepted sprites have visible 2-4 px paper-tooth speckle on every fill, uneven ink coverage and slightly softer, thinner keylines. This image is slick, vector-clean, with halo/ringing around keylines and smooth plastic fills (envelopes, hat box, lens cloth and parcel look airbrushed-clean). Placed next to the tin and hat box sprites it looks like a different artist. Grain is far below the bible 4-7 percent.
5. Meaningless micro-detail: the drawer fronts on the left carry small triangle-and-dot clusters (reads like braille), and a mesh patch on the window mullion and shelf uprights; none of it communicates anything.
6. The pull chain under the shade is an added object not in the round-1 composition; it is minor but the brief said keep objects as they were.

Verdict: not shippable as a store hero for an award-calibre game. Round 3 brief written.
