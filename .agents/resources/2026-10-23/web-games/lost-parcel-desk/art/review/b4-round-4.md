# Review: b4 round 4 (final)

## evidence-08-1 (rubber ferrule): REJECTED

Technical: 1024x1024 RGBA, corners and outer 6 percent border alpha 0, bbox 75.2 x 73.7 percent. These pass. The interior has about 700 transparent hole pixels, and about 6300 semi-transparent edge pixels, mostly antialiasing.

Reasons for rejection:
1. Purpose (rubric 8, brief's key test): at 54, 84 and 220 px it reads as a bucket hat, a thimble, or an oven/dial knob. It does not read as a rubber walking-stick tip. The barrel flares outward toward the base, so it is not the near-cylindrical cup the brief asked for. The tread marks look like dial tick marks on a knob. The silhouette is the same hat/thimble failure as round 3.
2. Construction (rubric 2): the lower-left silhouette is a long straight slanted edge that runs from the top ring to the base. It flares outward, the opposite of the requested inward chamfer. There is no foot shoulder step on the left, and the foot band does not read as a separate wider band. The left base corner ends in a hard vertical cut near x=115, so the object looks clipped. The worn face is a lighter, glossy-looking streak and not a flat plane. The inner edge line is a stray short seam that ends mid-body.
3. Style and AI tells (rubric 3, 7): the highlight is a pinkish diagonal glossy sheen with a curved lower boundary and a visible fibrous texture. The brief forbids this, and the flat matte bands on evidence-09-3 and evidence-10-1 do not look like it. There are random cream and light specks in the fill, which are meaningless micro-detail. The halftone covers well over 25 percent of the surface and runs across the dome and the full right side, so it reads heavier than the accepted sprites.
4. Shadow (brief item 6): the cast shadow is broken. It is a scatter of detached dots, with stray dots below the left base, a half-dot clipped by the body at the lower left, and dots with irregular spacing along the right edge. Several dots are cropped or merged with the keyline. The accepted sprites show a clean crescent of regular dots. This is the same fault as round 3.
5. Edge and technical quality (rubric 5): the keyline is faint or absent on the lower left and on the top-left of the dome, where a thin light fringe shows against the cream. Interior holes (about 700 px) break the rule that the silhouette interior is fully opaque.
6. Consistency (contact sheet c): beside the postcard, the 04-1 tin, 09-3 key and 10-1 knight, this asset has a different rendering. It has a glossy highlight, an uneven keyline and a ragged shadow, and it looks like it came from another set.

Round 4 is final, so the asset stays rejected and the slot should be filled by a placeholder or a manual redraw. No next brief has been written.
