# Street Royale Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-10-16**

Street Royale (package name `app.w3dev.citydrive`) is a single-player 3D city-driving game for
Android, iOS and web browsers. This page explains what the game does, and does not, do with your
data.

<Note>
This policy's hosting URL has not been assigned yet; publishing it (and filling in the support
contact below) is a human step before store submission. See the game's release checklist for
status.
</Note>

## Summary

- **No account is required.** You can open the game and play immediately.
- **No data is collected.** The game sends no personal information, device identifiers, usage
  statistics or crash reports to us or to anyone else.
- **No ads. No in-app purchases.** There is nothing to buy. Coins are earned by playing and cannot be
  bought.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no third-party services.**

## Information we collect

None. Street Royale has no account system, no way to identify you, and no code that reports
anything about you or your device. It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or
photos, and it does not ask for those permissions.

## Data stored on your device

Your progress (stars, coins, unlocked cars and districts, and your settings such as sound and
haptics) is saved on your device only:

- In the Android and iOS apps, in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.
- In the web version, in your browser's local storage for the game's web address.

This save never leaves your device through the game. You can remove it by uninstalling the app,
clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or clearing the site data in your browser.
There is no cloud sync, so progress does not carry over to another device.

If you have turned on your phone's own backup (for example Android's backup to your Google
account), your system may include the app's save file in that backup. That is done by your device
and your operating system provider under their terms, not by us, and we never receive it.

## Network access

The game makes no requests to any server of ours or of any third party while you play. The
Android app declares the `INTERNET` permission because the game screen runs inside Android's
WebView, which Android requires to hold that permission. The app uses it only to read its own
files bundled inside the app. All game art, sounds and fonts ship inside the app, and none are
loaded from the internet.

## The web version

When you open the web version, your browser downloads the game's files from our web host, and the
host's servers record standard technical request data (such as your IP address, the time and the
files requested) in ordinary server logs. We do not add cookies, analytics or advertising to the
page, and we do not use these logs to identify you.

## Advertising, analytics and purchases

Street Royale contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no purchase system. We do not show
ads, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or share data, because
we do not have any.

## Children's privacy

Street Royale does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
Because the game collects no personal information at all, it needs no special handling under
children's privacy laws such as COPPA. If that changes in a future version, this policy will be
updated first.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of Street Royale changes what it collects or how it behaves online (for
example by adding ads, analytics, online play or cloud saves), we will update this page and change
the "Last updated" date at the top before that version is released.
